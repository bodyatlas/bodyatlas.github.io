HotDocs alternative: lightweight document automation in the browser

HotDocs has been a standard for enterprise document assembly for decades. Clausery is a much lighter tool: no installation, no server, and no component library to learn, for firms that need the essentials.

Choose HotDocs if

  • You automate hundreds of complex, interlinked documents
  • You need enterprise administration, auditing and integrations
  • You have dedicated template authors

Choose Clausery if

  • You want to automate your ten most-used documents this afternoon
  • Nobody on the team should have to learn a template language
  • Budget or procurement rules out an enterprise contract

Try it with your own template

Open the app, drop in a Word document with tags like {client_name}, and you have a questionnaire. Or start from one of the free templates.
