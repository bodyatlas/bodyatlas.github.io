Clio Draft alternative for firms that cannot upload client files

Clio Draft (formerly Lawyaw) is document automation that lives inside the Clio ecosystem. Clausery is independent of any practice management system and never uploads the documents it builds.

Choose Clio Draft if

  • Your firm runs on Clio Manage and wants matter data to fill documents automatically
  • You want its court form library
  • You prefer one vendor for practice management and drafting

Choose Clausery if

  • You do not use Clio, or want drafting that works without it
  • You need client data to stay on firm devices
  • You want predictable pricing without a practice management subscription

Try it with your own template

Open the app, drop in a Word document with tags like {client_name}, and you have a questionnaire. Or start from one of the free templates.
