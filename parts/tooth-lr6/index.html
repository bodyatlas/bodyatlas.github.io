Lower right first molar

Dens molaris

SkeletalSkeletonHeadRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Large grinding tooth with four cusps around a central fossa and two roots (mesial and distal). The crown is covered with enamel, the hardest substance in the body.
Function
Grinds food into a paste during chewing.
Size
Crown ≈ 11.0 × 10.5 mm, 7.5 mm tall; root ≈ 14 mm
Clinical note
The first molar erupts at about 6 years (the "six-year molar") and is the key to the bite.
