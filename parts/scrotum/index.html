Scrotum

ReproductiveSkinPelvis

View in 3D

Shown on the male body.

What it is
Pouch of thin, wrinkled, pigmented skin containing the dartos smooth muscle, divided by a midline raphe and septum into two compartments, each holding a testis, epididymis and the lower spermatic cord.
Function
Keeps the testes 2–3 °C below body temperature by wrinkling or relaxing (dartos) and by the cremaster muscle raising or lowering them.
Size
About 7 cm across and 8–10 cm tall.
Clinical note
The raphe marks where the two labioscrotal swellings fused in the embryo; their female counterpart is the labia majora.
