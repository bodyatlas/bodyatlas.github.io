Lower left third molar

Dens molaris

SkeletalSkeletonHeadLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Large grinding tooth with four cusps around a central fossa and two roots (mesial and distal). The crown is covered with enamel, the hardest substance in the body.
Function
Grinds food into a paste during chewing.
Size
Crown ≈ 10.0 × 9.5 mm, 7.0 mm tall; root ≈ 11 mm
Clinical note
The wisdom tooth erupts at 17–25 years, if at all, and is often impacted.
