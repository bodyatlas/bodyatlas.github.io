Posterior semicircular canal

Canalis semicircularis posterior

What it is
The posterior semicircular canal lies in a vertical plane parallel to the long axis of the petrous bone, the most posterior of the three, with its ampulla at the lower end.
Function
Senses rotation in its plane: tilting the head toward the shoulder combined with pitch.
Size
Radius about 3.2-4 mm; the longest canal, about 20 mm.
Clinical note
The canal most often affected by benign positional vertigo, because loose otoconia settle into its lowest point.
