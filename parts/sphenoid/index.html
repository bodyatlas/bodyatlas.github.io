Sphenoid bone

Os sphenoidale

SkeletalSkeletonHead

View in 3D

What it is
Butterfly-shaped bone at the centre of the skull base: a hollow body with the sella turcica (seat of the pituitary), greater and lesser wings, and pterygoid plates hanging below.
Function
Keystone of the cranial base, joining with every other cranial bone; transmits the optic nerves and many other nerves and vessels.
Size
≈ 7 cm across the greater wings
Clinical note
The pituitary gland is reached surgically through the sphenoid sinus (transsphenoidal surgery).
