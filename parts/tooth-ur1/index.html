Upper right central incisor

Dens incisivus

SkeletalSkeletonHeadRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Chisel-shaped tooth with a thin cutting edge and a single conical root. The crown is covered with enamel, the hardest substance in the body.
Function
Bites and cuts food.
Size
Crown ≈ 8.5 × 7.0 mm, 10.5 mm tall; root ≈ 13 mm
Clinical note
Central incisors erupt at about 7 years (lower first) and are the teeth most often chipped in falls.
