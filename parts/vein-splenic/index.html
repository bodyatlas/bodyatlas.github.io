Splenic vein

Vena splenica (lienalis)

CardiovascularBlood vesselsAbdomenLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Leaves the hilum of the spleen and runs to the right in a groove on the back of the body of the pancreas, below the splenic artery, to join the superior mesenteric vein behind the pancreatic neck. It receives the inferior mesenteric vein.
Function
Drains the spleen, pancreas and part of the stomach into the portal system.
Size
~1 cm diameter.
Clinical note
Pancreatitis or pancreatic cancer can cause it to clot, producing isolated gastric varices.
