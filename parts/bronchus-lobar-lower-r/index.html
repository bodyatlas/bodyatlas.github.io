Right lower lobe bronchus

Bronchus lobaris inferior dexter

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Continues down and back from the bronchus intermedius, giving a superior segmental bronchus and four basal segmental bronchi.
Function
Secondary (lobar) bronchus: conducts air to one lobe of the lung.
Size
About 1–1.5 cm to the basal division, 8–10 mm wide.
Clinical note
Lobar bronchi are the level at which the lung is removed in a lobectomy; their walls contain irregular cartilage plates instead of rings.
