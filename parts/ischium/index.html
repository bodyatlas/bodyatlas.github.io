Ischium

Os ischii

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The lower posterior part of the hip bone: a thick body behind the acetabulum, the ischial spine, the rough ischial tuberosity, and a flat ramus that joins the pubis around the obturator foramen.
Function
Bears the body weight when sitting and anchors the hamstrings and adductor magnus.
Size
Tuberosity ≈ 5 cm long; inter-tuberous distance ≈ 11 cm
Clinical note
The ischial spines mark the narrowest part of the birth canal and guide pudendal nerve blocks.
