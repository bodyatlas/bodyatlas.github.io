Shown on the male body.

What it is
Single ventral erectile body surrounding the spongy urethra; it swells into the bulb in the perineum and expands at its far end into the glans.
Function
Engorges less than the corpora cavernosa, so the urethra stays open for ejaculation during erection.
Size
About 1 cm across in the shaft; the bulb is about 3 cm wide.
Clinical note
The bulbospongiosus muscle wraps its bulb and squeezes out the last drops of urine and semen.
