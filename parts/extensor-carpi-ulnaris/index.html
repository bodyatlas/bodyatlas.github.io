Extensor carpi ulnaris

Musculus extensor carpi ulnaris

MuscularSuperficial musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Arises from the lateral epicondyle and the posterior border of the ulna and runs down the back of the ulna to the base of the fifth metacarpal.
Function
Extends and adducts (ulnar-deviates) the wrist.
Size
About 24 cm long.
Clinical note
Its tendon can snap out of its groove over the ulnar head in tennis and golf players.
