Flexor digitorum profundus

Musculus flexor digitorum profundus

MuscularDeep musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Deep flexor from the upper anterior and medial ulna and the interosseous membrane; its four tendons run through the carpal tunnel beneath and then through the split superficialis tendons to the distal phalanges.
Function
The only muscle that bends the fingertips (distal interphalangeal joints); also flexes the other finger joints and the wrist.
Size
About 25 cm to the palm.
Clinical note
"Jersey finger" is avulsion of its ring-finger tendon when grabbing a shirt. The tendons are shown only as far as the knuckles.
