Right posterior segmental bronchus (B2)

Bronchus segmentalis posterior

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Segmental (tertiary) bronchus B2, supplying the posterior bronchopulmonary segment of the right upper lobe.
Function
Conducts air to one bronchopulmonary segment, a pyramidal unit of lung with its own bronchus and segmental artery.
Size
About 3 cm long and 4–6 mm in diameter; its wall holds irregular cartilage plates.
Clinical note
In a person lying on the right side, aspirated material drains into this segment.
