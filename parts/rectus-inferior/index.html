Inferior rectus

Musculus rectus inferior

Sensory (eye & ear)Deep musclesHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
A flat strap muscle arising from the common tendinous ring at the orbit apex and running forward along the orbital wall to insert by a thin, broad, white tendon on the sclera in front of the equator.
Function
Depresses the eye (mainly when it looks outward) and also turns it in and rotates it outward (extorsion).
Size
~40 mm long; inserts ~6.5 mm from the limbus.
Clinical note
It can be trapped in a blow-out fracture of the orbital floor, preventing upward gaze and causing double vision; enlarged in thyroid eye disease.
