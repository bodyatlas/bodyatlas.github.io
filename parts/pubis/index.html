Pubis

Os pubis

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The anterior part of the hip bone: a body meeting its partner at the pubic symphysis, with superior and inferior rami framing the obturator foramen and a pubic tubercle for the inguinal ligament.
Function
Completes the pelvic ring in front and anchors the adductors, rectus abdominis and inguinal ligament.
Size
Body ≈ 3 × 2 cm; symphysis ≈ 4 cm tall
Clinical note
The subpubic angle is wider in females (≈ 80–85°) than in males (≈ 50–60°).

Nearby skeletal structures Acetabulum, Coccyx, Ilium, Ischium, Pubic symphysis, Sacrum
