Flexor pollicis longus

Musculus flexor pollicis longus

MuscularDeep musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Deep flexor arising from the anterior surface of the radius and interosseous membrane; its tendon passes through the carpal tunnel to the distal phalanx of the thumb.
Function
Flexes the thumb, especially its tip joint.
Size
About 20 cm long.
Clinical note
Inability to bend the thumb tip (with an intact index fingertip) points to anterior interosseous nerve palsy. The tendon is shown only as far as the thumb's metacarpal.
