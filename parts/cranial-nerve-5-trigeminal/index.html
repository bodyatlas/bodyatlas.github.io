Trigeminal nerve (CN V), root

Nervus trigeminus (radix sensoria et motoria)

NervousNervesHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The largest cranial nerve. Its thick sensory root and small motor root leave the side of the pons and run forward over the petrous ridge to the trigeminal (Gasserian) ganglion in Meckel's cave.
Function
Sensation for the whole face, scalp to the vertex, cornea, teeth, mouth and the front two-thirds of the tongue; motor to the chewing muscles.
Size
Sensory root about 4-5 mm wide; 1.5 cm to the ganglion
Clinical note
Trigeminal neuralgia - lightning stabs of facial pain - is usually caused by a looping artery pressing on this root where it enters the pons.

Contains Trigeminal ganglion
