Semitendinosus

Musculus semitendinosus

MuscularSuperficial musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Superficial medial hamstring with a fleshy upper half and a long cord-like tendon. It arises from the ischial tuberosity with the biceps long head and inserts on the upper medial tibia (pes anserinus) behind gracilis.
Function
Extends the hip, flexes the knee and medially rotates the flexed knee.
Size
About 45 cm long including a tendon of roughly 20 cm.
Clinical note
Its tendon (often with gracilis) is the commonest graft used to reconstruct a torn anterior cruciate ligament.
