Neck of pancreas

Collum pancreatis

DigestiveOrgansAbdomen

View in 3D

What it is
Short narrowed part joining the head to the body, lying in front of the portal vein and superior mesenteric vessels.
Function
Its acinar cells secrete about 1.5 L a day of enzyme-rich alkaline juice into the duodenum; scattered islets of Langerhans release insulin and glucagon into the blood.
Size
About 2 cm long.
Clinical note
The portal vein forms right behind it (superior mesenteric + splenic veins), making it the key landmark in pancreatic surgery.
