Latissimus dorsi

Musculus latissimus dorsi

MuscularSuperficial musclesWhole bodyLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The broadest muscle of the back: a thin triangular sheet arising via the thoracolumbar fascia from the spinous processes of T7–L5 and the sacrum, the posterior iliac crest and the lower ribs. It sweeps up round the side of the chest, forming the posterior axillary fold, to a flat tendon in the floor of the intertubercular groove of the humerus.
Function
Extends, adducts and medially rotates the arm; pulls the trunk up towards the arms in climbing and helps in forced expiration and coughing.
Size
Up to ~40 cm across, 2–5 mm thick; tendon about 3 cm wide.
Clinical note
A workhorse flap for breast reconstruction; supplied by the thoracodorsal nerve.
