Extensor digitorum longus tendons

Tendines musculi extensoris digitorum longi

MuscularSuperficial musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
A common tendon passes under the extensor retinacula in front of the ankle and fans into four slips on the dorsum of the foot, forming the dorsal (extensor) expansions of toes 2-5.
Function
Lifts (extends) the lateral four toes.
Size
Common tendon about 5 mm; slips about 2 mm wide.
Clinical note
The slips stand out under the skin of the dorsum when the toes are raised - a quick test of the deep fibular nerve.
