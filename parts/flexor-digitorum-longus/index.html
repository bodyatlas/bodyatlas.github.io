Flexor digitorum longus

Musculus flexor digitorum longus

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Arises from the posterior surface of the tibia below the soleal line, on the medial side of the deep posterior compartment.
Function
Flexes the lateral four toes and helps plantarflex and invert the foot.
Size
Belly about 20 cm; about 35 cm with its tendons.
Clinical note
Just above the ankle it crosses superficial to tibialis posterior (the crural chiasm); its tendon is transferred to replace a failed tibialis posterior tendon.
