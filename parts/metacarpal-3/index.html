Third metacarpal

Os metacarpi III

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Long bone of the palm behind the middle finger, with a base at the carpus, a slender shaft and a rounded head forming the knuckle.
Function
Forms the skeleton of the palm and the knuckle joint of its finger.
Size
≈ 59 mm long
Clinical note
The metacarpal heads are the knuckles seen when making a fist.
