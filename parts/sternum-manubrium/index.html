Manubrium of sternum

Manubrium sterni

SkeletalSkeletonThorax

View in 3D

What it is
The broad upper part of the breastbone, with the jugular notch on top and notches for the clavicles and first ribs.
Function
Anchors the clavicles (sternoclavicular joints) and the first two pairs of costal cartilages.
Size
≈ 50 mm wide, 60 mm tall, 12 mm thick
Clinical note
Its joint with the sternal body forms the sternal angle (of Louis), the landmark for the 2nd rib and the tracheal bifurcation.
