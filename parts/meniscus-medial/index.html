Medial meniscus

Meniscus medialis

SkeletalSkeletonLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
C-shaped wedge of fibrocartilage on the medial tibial plateau, firmly attached to the joint capsule and medial collateral ligament.
Function
Deepens the tibial socket, spreads load and stabilises the knee.
Size
≈ 4.5 cm long, 9–10 mm wide
Clinical note
Tears more often than the lateral meniscus because it is less mobile (part of the "unhappy triad").
