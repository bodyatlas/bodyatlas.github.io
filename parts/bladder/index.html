Urinary bladder

Vesica urinaria

UrinaryOrgansPelvis

View in 3D

What it is
Hollow muscular reservoir behind the pubic symphysis. Its wall is the detrusor muscle (interlacing smooth-muscle bundles) lined by stretchy urothelium that folds into rugae when empty.
Function
Stores urine at low pressure and empties it during micturition, when the detrusor contracts and the urethral sphincters relax.
Size
Capacity 400–600 mL (urge at ~150–250 mL); shown moderately full, about 8 cm across.
Clinical note
As it fills it rises above the pubis into the extraperitoneal space, so a full bladder can be drained by a suprapubic catheter without entering the peritoneum.
