What it is
Blind pouch that begins the large intestine in the right iliac fossa, below the point where the ileum enters through the ileocecal valve; the appendix hangs from it.
Function
Receives liquid contents from the ileum and begins absorbing water and salts; the ileocecal valve limits backflow of colonic bacteria.
Size
About 6 cm long and 7.5 cm wide.
Clinical note
As the widest part of the colon it is the part most likely to split when the large bowel is obstructed (law of Laplace).
