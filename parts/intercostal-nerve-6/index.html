6th intercostal nerve

Nervus intercostalis VI

NervousNervesThoraxLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The ventral ramus of the 6th thoracic nerve. It runs round the chest in the costal groove on the underside of the 6th rib, between the internal and innermost intercostal muscles and below the intercostal vein and artery, ending beside the sternum as an anterior cutaneous branch.
Function
Motor to the intercostal muscles; sensation from a band of skin (lateral and anterior cutaneous branches) and from the parietal pleura.
Size
About 2-3 mm thick; 30-35 cm long
Clinical note
Shingles most often erupts along a mid-thoracic intercostal nerve as a one-sided painful band of blisters that stops at the midline.
