Trapezium

Os trapezium

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Distal-row bone at the base of the thumb with a saddle-shaped joint for the first metacarpal.
Function
Distal-row carpal: forms a rigid unit with the metacarpals and transmits grip forces to the forearm.
Size
≈ 13 × 12 × 12 mm
Clinical note
Its saddle joint gives the thumb opposition; it is a common site of osteoarthritis.
