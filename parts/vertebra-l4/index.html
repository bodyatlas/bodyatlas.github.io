L4 vertebra

Vertebra lumbalis L4

SkeletalSkeletonAbdomen

View in 3D

What it is
A lumbar vertebra: the largest vertebral bodies, kidney-shaped, with short hatchet-shaped spinous processes and long thin transverse processes.
Function
Bears most of the upper-body weight and allows bending forward and backward.
Size
Body 51 mm wide × 32 mm deep × 27 mm tall; canal ≈ 20 mm
Clinical note
Lumbar discs (L4–L5, L5–S1) are the commonest site of disc herniation and sciatica.
