Cephalic vein

Vena cephalica

CardiovascularBlood vesselsArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
A superficial vein that starts on the thumb side of the dorsal venous network of the hand, winds round to the front of the forearm, ascends lateral to biceps and runs in the deltopectoral groove before piercing the clavipectoral fascia to join the axillary vein.
Function
Drains superficial tissues of the lateral hand, forearm and arm.
Size
~3–5 mm diameter.
Clinical note
Easily visible at the wrist ("intern's vein") and used for cannulas; surgeons use it to insert pacemaker leads.

Nearby cardiovascular structures Basilic vein, Brachial artery, Median cubital vein, Radial artery, Superficial palmar arch and digital arteries, Ulnar artery
