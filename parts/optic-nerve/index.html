Optic nerve (intraorbital part)

Nervus opticus (pars orbitalis)

Sensory (eye & ear)NervesHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Cranial nerve II, a bundle of about 1.2 million retinal ganglion-cell axons. Its orbital part runs in a gentle S-curve from the back of the eyeball to the optic canal at the orbit apex; beyond the apex it continues to the optic chiasm.
Function
Carries visual information from the retina to the brain.
Size
Orbital part ~25 mm long (slack allows eye movement), 3-4 mm thick; whole nerve ~45-50 mm.
Clinical note
It is a tract of the central nervous system, so it does not regenerate; inflammation (optic neuritis) is often the first sign of multiple sclerosis.
