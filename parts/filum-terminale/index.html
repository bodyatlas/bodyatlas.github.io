Filum terminale

NervousOrgansWhole body

View in 3D

What it is
A thin thread of pia mater running from the tip of the conus medullaris down through the dural sac (internal part) and, wrapped in dura, on to the back of the coccyx (external part).
Function
Anchors the lower end of the spinal cord and steadies it within the vertebral canal.
Size
About 20 cm long and 1 mm thick
Clinical note
A short, thick filum (tethered cord) pulls on the conus as a child grows, causing back pain, leg weakness and bladder problems; it is treated by cutting the filum.
