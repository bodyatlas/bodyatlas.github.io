Cauda equina

NervousOrgansWhole body

View in 3D

What it is
The "horse's tail": the bundle of lumbar, sacral and coccygeal nerve roots (L2-Co1) that stream down the dural sac below the end of the cord to reach their exit foramina.
Function
Carries motor and sensory fibres for the legs, bladder, bowel and genitals.
Size
About 20 pairs of roots filling the lumbar dural sac from L1/L2 to S2 (~15-20 cm)
Clinical note
Compression by a large central disc herniation (cauda equina syndrome) causes saddle numbness and bladder retention - a surgical emergency.
