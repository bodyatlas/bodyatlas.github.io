Nasal bone

Os nasale

SkeletalSkeletonHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Small oblong bone forming, with its partner, the bony bridge of the nose; the lower edge carries the nasal cartilages.
Function
Supports the upper nose and protects the nasal cavity.
Size
≈ 2.5 cm long, 1 cm wide
Clinical note
The most commonly fractured facial bones.
