Tibialis anterior tendon

Tendo musculi tibialis anterioris

MuscularSuperficial musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Thick tendon crossing the front of the ankle, the most medial under the extensor retinacula, to the medial surface of the medial cuneiform and base of the first metatarsal.
Function
Transmits dorsiflexion and inversion to the medial foot.
Size
About 15 cm long, 6-7 mm diameter.
Clinical note
Easily seen and felt in front of the ankle when the foot is dorsiflexed and inverted; spontaneous rupture occurs in older adults.
