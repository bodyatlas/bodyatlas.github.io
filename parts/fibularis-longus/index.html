Fibularis longus

Musculus fibularis (peroneus) longus

What it is
Superficial muscle of the lateral compartment, arising from the head and upper two-thirds of the lateral fibula (also called peroneus longus).
Function
Everts the foot and plantarflexes the ankle; with tibialis anterior it forms a "stirrup" under the foot that supports the transverse arch.
Size
Belly about 20 cm long.
Clinical note
The common fibular nerve winds around the fibular neck deep to its origin - a vulnerable spot for compression by casts, braces or leg-crossing.
