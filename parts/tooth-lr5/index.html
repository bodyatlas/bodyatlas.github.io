Lower right second premolar

Dens premolaris

SkeletalSkeletonHeadRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Transitional tooth with two cusps (buccal and lingual) on its biting surface and a single root. The crown is covered with enamel, the hardest substance in the body.
Function
Holds and crushes food.
Size
Crown ≈ 7.0 × 8.0 mm, 8.0 mm tall; root ≈ 15 mm
Clinical note
Premolars replace the deciduous molars at 10–12 years.
