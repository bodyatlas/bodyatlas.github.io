Scala vestibuli

Sensory (eye & ear)OrgansHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The upper perilymph-filled channel of the cochlea, running from the oval window to the apex above the cochlear duct, from which it is separated by the very thin Reissner's (vestibular) membrane.
Function
Carries the pressure wave from the stapes footplate toward the apex.
Size
Follows all 2.5 turns; joins the scala tympani through the helicotrema at the apex.
Clinical note
Perilymph resembles cerebrospinal fluid (sodium-rich) and communicates with the subarachnoid space through the cochlear aqueduct.
