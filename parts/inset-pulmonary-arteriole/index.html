Pulmonary arteriole (magnified)

Arteriola pulmonalis

RespiratoryOrgansWhole bodyRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Branch of the pulmonary artery that runs beside the bronchiole and divides with it to feed the alveolar capillaries; shown blue because it carries deoxygenated blood.
Function
Delivers blood from the right ventricle to the gas-exchange surface.
Size
Real pulmonary arterioles are ~15–100 µm in diameter.
Clinical note
Unlike systemic arterioles, pulmonary arterioles constrict when alveolar oxygen is low (hypoxic vasoconstriction), diverting blood to well-ventilated lung.
