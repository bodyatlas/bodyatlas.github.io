Humeral head cartilage

Cartilago articularis capitis humeri

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Smooth hyaline cartilage covering the articular surface of the humeral head.
Function
Lets the head glide in the glenoid with almost no friction.
Size
≈ 1–2 mm thick
Clinical note
The glenoid is only a third as large as the head, so the shoulder is the most commonly dislocated major joint.
