Gluteus medius

Musculus gluteus medius

MuscularSuperficial musclesPelvisLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Thick fan-shaped muscle on the outer surface of the ilium between the anterior and posterior gluteal lines, partly covered by gluteus maximus; its fibres converge on the lateral surface of the greater trochanter.
Function
Main abductor of the hip; in walking it keeps the pelvis level when the opposite foot is off the ground. Its anterior fibres also rotate the hip medially.
Size
About 15 cm wide at its origin, 2 cm thick.
Clinical note
Weakness or superior gluteal nerve injury gives a positive Trendelenburg sign: the pelvis drops on the unsupported side during single-leg stance.
