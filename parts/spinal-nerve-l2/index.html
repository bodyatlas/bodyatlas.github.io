L2 spinal nerve

Nervus spinalis L2

NervousNervesAbdomenLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The L2 spinal nerve leaves the spinal canal between L2 and L3. Its sensory (dorsal) and motor (ventral) roots meet just beyond the dorsal root ganglion; the short mixed nerve then splits into a small dorsal ramus for the back muscles and skin and a larger ventral ramus, which enters the lumbar plexus inside psoas.
Function
Carries motor fibres to the muscles of its segment (myotome) and sensation back from its strip of skin (dermatome): the upper front of the thigh.
Size
About 4-5 mm thick; the mixed nerve is only ~1 cm long before it divides
Clinical note
Test: hip flexion (iliopsoas); it contributes to the femoral, obturator and lateral femoral cutaneous nerves.
