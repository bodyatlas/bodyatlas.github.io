Right hepatic duct

Ductus hepaticus dexter

DigestiveOrgansAbdomenRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Collects bile from the functional right half of the liver (segments V-VIII) and joins the left hepatic duct at the porta hepatis.
Function
Drains bile out of the right liver.
Size
About 1 cm long outside the liver, 3-4 mm wide.
Clinical note
Variations in how the right-sided ducts join are common, a known hazard during gallbladder surgery.
