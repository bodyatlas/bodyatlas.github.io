Third metatarsal

Os metatarsi III

SkeletalSkeletonLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Long bone of the forefoot behind the third toe, with a wedge-shaped base, slender shaft and rounded head at the ball of the foot.
Function
Forms a spoke of the foot's arches and a lever for walking.
Size
≈ 73 mm long
Clinical note
Bunions (hallux valgus) develop at the first metatarsal head.
