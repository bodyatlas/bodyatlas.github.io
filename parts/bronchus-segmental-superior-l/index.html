Left superior segmental bronchus (B6)

Bronchus segmentalis superior

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Segmental (tertiary) bronchus B6, supplying the superior bronchopulmonary segment of the left lower lobe.
Function
Conducts air to one bronchopulmonary segment, a pyramidal unit of lung with its own bronchus and segmental artery.
Size
About 3 cm long and 4–6 mm in diameter; its wall holds irregular cartilage plates.
Clinical note
The superior segment of the lower lobe is the commonest site of aspiration pneumonia in a patient lying on the back.
