Adductor brevis

Musculus adductor brevis

MuscularDeep musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Short triangular adductor lying behind pectineus and adductor longus, from the body and inferior ramus of the pubis to the upper third of the linea aspera.
Function
Adducts the hip and helps flex it.
Size
About 12-15 cm long.
Clinical note
The obturator nerve divides around it - the anterior division runs in front of it and the posterior division behind it.
