Left gastric artery

Arteria gastrica sinistra

CardiovascularBlood vesselsAbdomen

View in 3D

What it is
The smallest coeliac branch; it climbs to the cardia of the stomach, gives oesophageal branches, then runs down the lesser curvature to join the right gastric artery.
Function
Supplies the lesser curvature of the stomach and the lower oesophagus.
Size
~3–4 mm diameter.
Clinical note
A bleeding gastric ulcer high on the lesser curvature can erode it, causing major haematemesis.
