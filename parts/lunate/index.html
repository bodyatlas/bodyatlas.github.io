Lunate

Os lunatum

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Crescent-shaped bone in the centre of the proximal row, articulating with the radius.
Function
Proximal-row carpal: adapts its position as the wrist bends so the hand moves smoothly on the forearm.
Size
≈ 12 × 14 × 15 mm
Clinical note
The most commonly dislocated carpal bone; Kienböck disease is its avascular necrosis.
