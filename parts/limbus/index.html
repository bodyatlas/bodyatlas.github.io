Corneoscleral limbus

Limbus corneae

Sensory (eye & ear)OrgansHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The 1-2 mm wide grey-blue transition zone where the clear cornea meets the white sclera. It houses the corneal stem cells and, on its inner side, the drainage angle with the trabecular meshwork and Schlemm's canal.
Function
Regenerates the corneal surface and drains aqueous humour out of the eye.
Size
Ring ~11.7 mm across and 1-2 mm wide.
Clinical note
Surgeons enter the eye at the limbus for cataract and glaucoma operations; a grey ring just inside it (arcus) can signal high cholesterol in younger adults.

Nearby sensory (eye & ear) structures Anterior chamber (aqueous humour), Ciliary body, Cornea, Crystalline lens (cortex), Iris, Lens capsule, Lens nucleus, Pupil, Schlemm's canal, Zonular fibres (suspensory ligament of the lens)
