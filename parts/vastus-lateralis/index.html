Vastus lateralis

Musculus vastus lateralis

What it is
Largest head of the quadriceps, forming the bulk of the outer thigh. It arises from the greater trochanter, intertrochanteric line, gluteal tuberosity and lateral lip of the linea aspera, and inserts through the quadriceps tendon on the lateral border of the patella.
Function
Extends the knee.
Size
About 40 cm long and up to 8 cm wide; roughly 3 cm thick at mid-thigh.
Clinical note
Common site for intramuscular injections in infants and for muscle biopsies; its lateral pull on the patella is balanced by vastus medialis.

Nearby muscular structures Adductor brevis, Adductor longus, Adductor magnus, Biceps femoris (long head), Gracilis, Rectus femoris, Semimembranosus, Semitendinosus, Vastus intermedius, Vastus medialis
