Laryngopharynx

Pars laryngea pharyngis

RespiratoryOrgansNeck

View in 3D

What it is
The lowest part of the pharynx (hypopharynx), from the epiglottis to the cricoid cartilage, lying behind the larynx with a piriform recess on either side.
Function
Channels swallowed food around the laryngeal inlet into the oesophagus.
Size
About 4–5 cm long, narrowing to ~1.5 cm at the upper oesophageal sphincter (level of C6).
Clinical note
Fish bones often lodge in the piriform recesses; a Zenker's diverticulum bulges through its back wall just above the cricopharyngeus.

Nearby respiratory structures Arytenoid cartilage, Cricoid cartilage, Epiglottis, Laryngeal cavity, Thyroid cartilage, Vocal fold
