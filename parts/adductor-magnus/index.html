Adductor magnus

Musculus adductor magnus

MuscularDeep musclesLegLeft and right

What it is
The largest adductor: a huge triangular sheet from the inferior pubic ramus, ischial ramus and ischial tuberosity to the whole length of the linea aspera (adductor part) and the adductor tubercle above the medial femoral condyle (hamstring part).
Function
Adducts the hip; its adductor part also flexes and its hamstring part extends the hip.
Size
About 30 cm long and up to 15 cm wide.
Clinical note
The adductor hiatus in its lower tendon lets the femoral vessels pass to the back of the knee, where they become the popliteal vessels; dual nerve supply (obturator and tibial).
