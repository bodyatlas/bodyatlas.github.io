Right brachiocephalic vein

Vena brachiocephalica dextra

CardiovascularBlood vesselsThoraxRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Formed behind the right sternoclavicular joint by the right internal jugular and subclavian veins; descends almost vertically to unite with the left brachiocephalic vein as the superior vena cava.
Function
Drains the right side of the head, neck and right arm; receives the right lymphatic duct.
Size
~2.5 cm long, ~1.2 cm wide.
Clinical note
Its straight line into the SVC makes the right internal jugular the preferred route for central lines and pacing wires.
