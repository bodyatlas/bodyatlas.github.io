Transverse colon

Colon transversum

DigestiveOrgansAbdomen

View in 3D

What it is
Most mobile part of the colon, slung on the transverse mesocolon from the hepatic flexure to the higher splenic flexure and sagging below the stomach; the greater omentum hangs from it.
Function
Continues water absorption and bacterial fermentation, compacting the contents into faeces.
Size
About 45 cm long and about 5 cm wide.
Clinical note
The region near the splenic flexure is a watershed between the superior and inferior mesenteric arteries, so it is vulnerable to ischaemic colitis.
