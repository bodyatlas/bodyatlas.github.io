Pronator quadratus

Musculus pronator quadratus

MuscularDeep musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Flat square muscle deep in the lower forearm, stretching across from the lower quarter of the front of the ulna to that of the radius.
Function
The prime mover of pronation; also binds the lower ends of radius and ulna together.
Size
About 4–5 cm square and ~5 mm thick.
Clinical note
It is lifted off the radius in the standard volar approach for plating distal radius fractures.
