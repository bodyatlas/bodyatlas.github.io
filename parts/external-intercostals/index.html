External intercostal muscles

Musculi intercostales externi

MuscularDeep musclesWhole bodyLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Eleven thin sheets, one in each intercostal space, whose fibres run from the lower border of one rib down and forwards to the upper border of the rib below; they reach from the rib tubercles to the costochondral junctions, beyond which the external intercostal membrane continues.
Function
Raise the ribs in inspiration and stop the intercostal spaces bulging or being sucked in.
Size
Each space ~1.5–2 cm tall; sheets ~3 mm thick.
Clinical note
The intercostal nerve and vessels run under the rib above, so chest drains and needles are passed just over the upper border of a rib.
