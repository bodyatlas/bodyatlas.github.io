Hamate

Os hamatum

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Wedge-shaped distal-row bone on the little-finger side with a palmar hook (hamulus).
Function
Distal-row carpal: forms a rigid unit with the metacarpals and transmits grip forces to the forearm.
Size
≈ 14 × 17 × 14 mm
Clinical note
Its hook forms a wall of Guyon's canal and can break in golf or baseball swings.
