Hypothenar muscles

Eminentia hypothenaris (mm. abductor, flexor et opponens digiti minimi)

MuscularSuperficial musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Muscles forming the little-finger side of the palm: abductor, flexor and opponens digiti minimi, from the pisiform, hamate and flexor retinaculum to the fifth metacarpal and base of the little finger.
Function
Abducts and flexes the little finger and cups the palm.
Size
About 5 cm long and 2 cm wide.
Clinical note
Supplied by the ulnar nerve; their wasting suggests ulnar nerve damage at the elbow or in Guyon's canal.
