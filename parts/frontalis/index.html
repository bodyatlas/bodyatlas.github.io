Frontalis

Venter frontalis musculi occipitofrontalis

What it is
Frontal belly of the occipitofrontalis: a thin quadrilateral sheet over the forehead that arises from the galea aponeurotica (epicranial aponeurosis) and inserts into the skin of the eyebrow, blending with orbicularis oculi. It has no bony attachment.
Function
Raises the eyebrows and wrinkles the forehead horizontally.
Size
About 6 cm tall and 5 cm wide per side, 1–2 mm thick.
Clinical note
Supplied by the temporal branch of the facial nerve: in Bell's palsy the forehead cannot be wrinkled on that side, whereas it is spared in a stroke.

Nearby muscular structures Buccinator, Masseter, Orbicularis oculi, Orbicularis oris, Temporalis, Zygomaticus major
