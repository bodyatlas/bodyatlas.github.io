Left pulmonary artery

Arteria pulmonalis sinistra

CardiovascularBlood vesselsThoraxLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
The shorter branch: passes to the left in front of the descending aorta and arches over the left main bronchus into the hilum of the left lung; the ligamentum arteriosum links its origin to the aortic arch.
Function
Carries deoxygenated blood to the left lung.
Size
~3 cm long before dividing, ~2 cm diameter.
Clinical note
In the fetus the ductus arteriosus shunted blood from here into the aorta, bypassing the unexpanded lungs; it closes within days of birth.
