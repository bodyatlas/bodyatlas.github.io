Frontal sinus

Sinus frontalis

RespiratoryOrgansHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Air-filled space in the frontal bone behind the eyebrow, usually asymmetric and separated from its partner by a thin bony septum.
Function
Lightens the skull, adds resonance to the voice and may act as a crumple zone protecting the brain.
Size
About 3 cm high, 2.5 cm wide and 2 cm deep; absent at birth, it develops after about age 7.
Clinical note
It drains through the frontonasal duct into the middle meatus; infection can spread to the orbit or through the thin posterior wall to the brain.
