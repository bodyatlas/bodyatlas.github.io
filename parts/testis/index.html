Shown on the male body.

What it is
Oval gonad hanging in the scrotum on the spermatic cord, enclosed in the white fibrous tunica albuginea and divided into about 250 lobules packed with seminiferous tubules.
Function
Produces sperm (about 100 million a day) and the hormone testosterone from its interstitial Leydig cells.
Size
About 4.5 × 2.5 × 3 cm, 15–25 mL.
Clinical note
Kept 2–3 °C below core temperature; the left usually hangs a little lower. An undescended testis raises the risk of infertility and cancer.
