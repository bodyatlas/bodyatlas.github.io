Oesophageal hiatus

Hiatus oesophageus

MuscularDeep musclesThorax

View in 3D

What it is
Oval opening in the muscular part of the diaphragm at about T10, formed by a sling of right-crus fibres, transmitting the oesophagus and the vagal trunks.
Function
Its muscular sling acts as an external lower oesophageal sphincter, helping to prevent acid reflux.
Size
About 2.5 × 1.5 cm.
Clinical note
Widening of the hiatus lets the top of the stomach slide into the chest — a sliding hiatus hernia, a common cause of heartburn.
