Uterus

ReproductiveOrgansPelvis

View in 3D

Shown on the female body.

What it is
Thick-walled, pear-shaped muscular organ between the bladder and the rectum, normally tipped forward over the bladder (anteverted and anteflexed). Its dome-shaped fundus lies above the openings of the uterine tubes, above the body and isthmus.
Function
Receives the embryo, nourishes the growing fetus and expels it by powerful myometrial contractions in labour; sheds its lining each month as menstruation.
Size
About 7.5 × 5 × 2.5 cm and 50–70 g in a woman who has not given birth; the wall (myometrium) is 1.5–2 cm thick.
Clinical note
In pregnancy it grows to about 1 kg and reaches the ribs; fibroids (leiomyomas) are common benign tumours of its muscle.

Contains Endometrium, Uterine cavity and cervical canal

Nearby reproductive structures Cervix, Clitoris, Fimbriae of the uterine tube, Labium majus, Labium minus, Ovary, Uterine (fallopian) tube, Vagina
