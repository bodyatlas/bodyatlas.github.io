Sacrum

Os sacrum

View in 3D

What it is
Wedge-shaped bone of five fused sacral vertebrae, curved concave-forward, with four pairs of sacral foramina and a median crest behind.
Function
Transmits the weight of the trunk to the hip bones through the sacroiliac joints and forms the back wall of the pelvis.
Size
≈ 105 mm wide at the base, 110 mm tall
Clinical note
The sacral promontory (front edge of S1) is an obstetric landmark for measuring the pelvic inlet.
