Lower right lateral incisor

Dens incisivus

SkeletalSkeletonHeadRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Chisel-shaped tooth with a thin cutting edge and a single conical root. The crown is covered with enamel, the hardest substance in the body.
Function
Bites and cuts food.
Size
Crown ≈ 5.9 × 6.3 mm, 9.5 mm tall; root ≈ 14 mm
Clinical note
Upper lateral incisors are the most often congenitally missing teeth after wisdom teeth.
