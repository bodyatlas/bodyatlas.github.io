Aortic hiatus (median arcuate ligament)

Hiatus aorticus

MuscularDeep musclesThorax

View in 3D

What it is
Arched opening behind the diaphragm at T12, bounded in front by the median arcuate ligament that joins the two crura across the vertebral column; it transmits the aorta, the thoracic duct and often the azygos vein.
Function
Lets the aorta pass behind the muscle rather than through it, so breathing does not squeeze it.
Size
About 2.5–3 cm across.
Clinical note
A low-lying median arcuate ligament can compress the coeliac trunk, causing pain after meals (median arcuate ligament syndrome).
