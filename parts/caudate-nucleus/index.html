Caudate nucleus

Nucleus caudatus

NervousOrgansHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
A C-shaped nucleus with a large head bulging into the anterior horn of the lateral ventricle, a body arching back along the ventricle wall above the thalamus and a thin tail curving down into the temporal lobe.
Function
Part of the striatum: helps select and start wanted movements and behaviours and suppress unwanted ones, and supports habit and reward learning.
Size
Head about 1.5-2 cm across; about 10 cm long along its curve
Clinical note
In Huntington disease the caudate head wastes away so the anterior horns look ballooned on scans, producing chorea and dementia.

Nearby nervous structures Cerebral white matter, Dura mater (cranial), Fornix, Globus pallidus, Hippocampus, Lateral ventricle, Putamen, Temporal lobe, Thalamus, Third ventricle
