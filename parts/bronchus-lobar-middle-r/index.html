Right middle lobe bronchus

Bronchus lobaris medius

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Runs forwards and laterally from the bronchus intermedius and divides into lateral and medial segmental bronchi.
Function
Secondary (lobar) bronchus: conducts air to one lobe of the lung.
Size
About 1.5–2 cm long, 5–7 mm wide.
Clinical note
Lobar bronchi are the level at which the lung is removed in a lobectomy; their walls contain irregular cartilage plates instead of rings.
