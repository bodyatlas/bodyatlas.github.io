Fibula

What it is
Slender lateral bone of the leg with a knob-like head below the knee and a lower end forming the lateral malleolus of the ankle.
Function
Carries little weight (≈ 15%) but anchors leg muscles and stabilises the ankle mortise.
Size
≈ 40 cm long, 1–1.5 cm thick
Clinical note
The common fibular nerve winds around its neck, where it is easily injured, causing foot drop.
