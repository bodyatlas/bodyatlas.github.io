Right adrenal gland

Glandula suprarenalis dextra

Endocrine & glandsGlandsAbdomenRight side

View in 3D

What it is
A pyramid-shaped gland perched on the top of the right kidney, wedged between the liver, the inferior vena cava and the diaphragm. Shown with a wedge cut away to reveal its outer cortex and inner medulla. Its thin fibrous capsule is what you see here.
Function
The capsule binds the cortex and medulla into one organ and carries the dozens of small arteries that pierce it from the aorta, phrenic and renal arteries.
Size
About 3 × 3.5 × 1 cm, 4–5 g
Clinical note
The right adrenal vein is only a few millimetres long and enters the vena cava directly, which makes right adrenal surgery bloodier than the left.
