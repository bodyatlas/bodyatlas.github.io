Vastus intermedius

Musculus vastus intermedius

What it is
Deepest head of the quadriceps, wrapped directly around the front and outer side of the femoral shaft beneath rectus femoris. It arises from the upper two-thirds of the anterior and lateral femur and inserts through the deep layer of the quadriceps tendon.
Function
Extends the knee.
Size
About 30 cm long, 1.5-2 cm thick.
Clinical note
A small slip (articularis genus) lifts the suprapatellar bursa during extension; a "dead leg" contusion crushes it against the femur and can lead to myositis ossificans.
