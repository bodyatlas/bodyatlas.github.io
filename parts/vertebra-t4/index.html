T4 vertebra

Vertebra thoracica T4

SkeletalSkeletonThorax

View in 3D

What it is
A thoracic vertebra: heart-shaped body with costal facets for the ribs, long spinous process angled downward and sturdy transverse processes.
Function
Anchors the ribs and protects the spinal cord while allowing limited rotation of the chest.
Size
Body 32 mm wide × 24 mm deep × 22 mm tall; canal ≈ 16 mm
Clinical note
Overlapping, downward-sloping spines limit extension of the thoracic spine.
