Sural nerve

Nervus suralis

NervousNervesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
A cutaneous nerve formed by the medial sural branch of the tibial nerve and a communicating branch from the common fibular nerve. It descends just under the skin down the back of the calf beside the small saphenous vein, passes behind the lateral malleolus and runs along the outer edge of the foot to the little toe.
Function
Sensation from the lower back of the calf, the outer ankle and the lateral border of the foot.
Size
About 2 mm thick; ~40 cm long
Clinical note
Losing it only numbs a strip of the foot, so it is the standard donor for nerve grafts and the nerve biopsied to diagnose peripheral neuropathy.

Part of Tibial nerve
