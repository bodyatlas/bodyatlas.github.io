Scaphoid

Os scaphoideum

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Boat-shaped bone of the proximal row on the thumb side, bridging both carpal rows.
Function
Proximal-row carpal: adapts its position as the wrist bends so the hand moves smoothly on the forearm.
Size
≈ 11 × 19 × 11 mm
Clinical note
The most frequently fractured carpal bone; its blood supply enters distally, so the proximal pole can die (avascular necrosis).
