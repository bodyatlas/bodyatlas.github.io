Kidney

Ren

What it is
Bean-shaped retroperitoneal organ lying on the posterior abdominal wall between T12 and L3, wrapped in a tough fibrous capsule and perirenal fat. Its hilum, facing anteromedially, transmits the renal artery, vein and pelvis.
Function
Filters about 180 L of plasma a day into 1–2 L of urine, regulating water, salt, potassium, acid–base balance and blood pressure, and secretes renin, erythropoietin and active vitamin D.
Size
About 11 × 6 × 3 cm, 120–170 g each; ~1 million nephrons per kidney.
Clinical note
The right kidney usually sits 1–2 cm lower than the left because the liver lies above it. Both move 2–3 cm with breathing.

Contains Renal calyces (right kidney), Renal cortex (right kidney, cut), Renal pelvis (right), Renal pyramids (right kidney)
