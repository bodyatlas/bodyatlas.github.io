Right lung, upper lobe

Lobus superior pulmonis dextri

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxRight side

View in 3D

What it is
The right upper lobe fills the apex and upper front of the right lung, above the horizontal fissure and in front of the upper part of the oblique fissure.
Function
Gas exchange; it is ventilated by the right upper lobe bronchus through its apical, posterior and anterior segments.
Size
About 20% of total lung volume; its apex rises 2–3 cm above the medial third of the clavicle.
Clinical note
Because the apex projects into the root of the neck, subclavian line insertion or a stab wound there can cause a pneumothorax; apical (Pancoast) tumours can compress the brachial plexus and sympathetic chain.

Part of Visceral pleura
