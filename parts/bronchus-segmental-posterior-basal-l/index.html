Left posterior basal segmental bronchus (B10)

Bronchus segmentalis posterior basal

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Segmental (tertiary) bronchus B10, supplying the posterior basal bronchopulmonary segment of the left lower lobe.
Function
Conducts air to one bronchopulmonary segment, a pyramidal unit of lung with its own bronchus and segmental artery.
Size
About 3 cm long and 4–6 mm in diameter; its wall holds irregular cartilage plates.
Clinical note
Because each segment has its own airway and blood supply, a diseased segment can be removed on its own (segmentectomy).
