What it is
Flat quadrangular muscle from the pectineal line (pecten) of the superior pubic ramus to the pectineal line of the femur just below the lesser trochanter; it forms part of the floor of the femoral triangle.
Function
Adducts and flexes the hip.
Size
About 10-12 cm long and 4-5 cm wide.
Clinical note
Often has a dual nerve supply (femoral and obturator); it lies directly behind the femoral vessels and femoral canal, the site of femoral hernias.
