Left upper lobe bronchus

Bronchus lobaris superior sinister

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Leaves the left main bronchus at the hilum and divides into an upper division (apicoposterior and anterior) and a lingular division.
Function
Secondary (lobar) bronchus: conducts air to one lobe of the lung.
Size
About 1–1.5 cm long, 8–10 mm wide.
Clinical note
Lobar bronchi are the level at which the lung is removed in a lobectomy; their walls contain irregular cartilage plates instead of rings.
