Pubic symphysis

Symphysis pubica

SkeletalSkeletonPelvis

View in 3D

What it is
Fibrocartilaginous disc joining the two pubic bones in the midline.
Function
Allows slight movement and absorbs stress in the pelvic ring.
Size
≈ 4 cm tall, 4 mm wide
Clinical note
Pregnancy hormones (relaxin) loosen it, widening the pelvis slightly for childbirth.

Nearby skeletal structures Acetabulum, Coccyx, Ilium, Ischium, Pubis, Sacrum
