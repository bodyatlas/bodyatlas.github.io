Triquetrum

Os triquetrum

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Pyramidal bone on the little-finger side of the proximal row.
Function
Proximal-row carpal: adapts its position as the wrist bends so the hand moves smoothly on the forearm.
Size
≈ 11 × 12 × 12 mm
Clinical note
Second most commonly fractured carpal, often as a dorsal chip.

Nearby skeletal structures Capitate, Fifth metacarpal, First metacarpal, Fourth metacarpal, Hamate, Lunate, Pisiform, Scaphoid, Trapezium, Trapezoid
