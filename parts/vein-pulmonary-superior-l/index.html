Left superior pulmonary vein

Vena pulmonalis sinistra superior

CardiovascularBlood vesselsThoraxLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
The superior left pulmonary vein leaves the upper part of the left lung hilum and runs a short course to open into the posterior wall of the left atrium.
Function
Returns oxygenated blood from the lungs to the left side of the heart.
Size
~1–1.5 cm diameter; 1–2 cm long outside the lung.
Clinical note
Sleeves of atrial muscle extending into the pulmonary veins trigger most atrial fibrillation, the target of catheter "pulmonary vein isolation".
