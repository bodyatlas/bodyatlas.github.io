C2 vertebra

Vertebra cervicalis C2

SkeletalSkeletonHead

View in 3D

What it is
The axis: carries the dens (odontoid peg), which projects up through the ring of the atlas and is held by the transverse ligament.
Function
Acts as the pivot for about half of all head rotation.
Size
Body 23 mm wide × 16 mm deep × 14 mm tall; canal ≈ 18 mm
Clinical note
A "hangman's fracture" breaks both pedicles of C2.
