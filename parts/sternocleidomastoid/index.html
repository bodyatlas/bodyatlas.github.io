Sternocleidomastoid

Musculus sternocleidomastoideus

MuscularSuperficial musclesWhole bodyLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Strap muscle crossing the side of the neck obliquely, from the mastoid process and superior nuchal line down to two heads: a rounded tendinous sternal head on the manubrium and a flat fleshy clavicular head on the medial third of the clavicle.
Function
Acting alone it turns the face to the opposite side and tilts the head to the same side; both together flex the neck and help lift the thorax in forced inspiration.
Size
About 16 cm long and 3–4 cm wide at mid-neck.
Clinical note
Fibrosis of it in infancy causes congenital torticollis (wry neck); it divides the neck into the anterior and posterior triangles.
