Achilles tendon

Tendo calcaneus

What it is
The calcaneal tendon, thickest and strongest tendon in the body, formed by the union of gastrocnemius and soleus and inserting on the middle of the back of the calcaneus.
Function
Transmits the force of the triceps surae to plantarflex the foot in walking, running and jumping - loads of several times body weight.
Size
About 15 cm long; about 1.5 cm wide and 6 mm thick at its narrowest.
Clinical note
Usually ruptures 2-6 cm above its insertion where the blood supply is poorest; the Thompson (calf-squeeze) test detects a complete tear.
