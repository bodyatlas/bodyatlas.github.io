Extensor hallucis longus tendon

Tendo musculi extensoris hallucis longi

MuscularDeep musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Crosses the front of the ankle between the tendons of tibialis anterior and extensor digitorum longus and runs along the dorsum of the first metatarsal to the base of the distal phalanx of the great toe.
Function
Extends the great toe.
Size
About 15 cm long, 4 mm diameter.
Clinical note
Easily cut by lacerations on the top of the foot, leaving the big toe drooping.
