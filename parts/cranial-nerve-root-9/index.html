Glossopharyngeal nerve root (CN IX)

Radix nervi glossopharyngei

NervousOrgansHead

View in 3D

What it is
The uppermost of a row of rootlets leaving the postolivary sulcus of the medulla, behind the olive. Shown as a short stub on both sides; the nerve continues in the peripheral nervous system.
Function
Taste and sensation from the back of the tongue and pharynx; carotid sinus and body reflexes; parotid secretion.
Size
Stub about 1 cm; root 1.8 mm thick
Clinical note
Leaves the skull through the jugular foramen with X and XI, so tumours there affect all three.
