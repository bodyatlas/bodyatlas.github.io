C6 vertebra

Vertebra cervicalis C6

SkeletalSkeletonNeck

View in 3D

What it is
A cervical (neck) vertebra: small oval body, large triangular canal and transverse processes pierced by foramina for the vertebral artery.
Function
Supports the head and gives the neck its wide range of flexion, extension and rotation.
Size
Body 27 mm wide × 16 mm deep × 14 mm tall; canal ≈ 18 mm
Clinical note
Cervical vertebrae C3–C6 have short bifid (forked) spinous processes.
