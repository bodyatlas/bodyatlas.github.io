Coracobrachialis

Musculus coracobrachialis

MuscularDeep musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Slender muscle from the tip of the coracoid process (sharing a tendon with the short head of biceps) to the medial surface of the middle of the humerus.
Function
Flexes and adducts the arm at the shoulder.
Size
About 15 cm long, 2 cm wide.
Clinical note
Pierced by the musculocutaneous nerve — a landmark when blocking that nerve in the axilla.

Nearby muscular structures Anconeus, Biceps brachii, Brachialis, Brachioradialis, Deltoid, Extensor carpi radialis longus, Flexor carpi ulnaris, Pronator teres, Supinator, Triceps brachii
