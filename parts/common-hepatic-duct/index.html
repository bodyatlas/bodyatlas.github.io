Common hepatic duct

Ductus hepaticus communis

DigestiveOrgansAbdomen

View in 3D

What it is
Formed at the porta hepatis by the union of the right and left hepatic ducts; descends in the free edge of the lesser omentum to meet the cystic duct.
Function
Carries all the bile leaving the liver.
Size
About 3 cm long and 6 mm wide.
Clinical note
With the cystic duct and the liver it bounds the hepatocystic (Calot's) triangle, which surgeons clear before clipping the cystic duct and artery.
