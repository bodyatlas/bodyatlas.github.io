Talus

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The ankle bone: a domed trochlea gripped by the malleoli above, a neck and a rounded head that fits the navicular.
Function
Transmits the entire body weight from the tibia to the foot; no muscles attach to it.
Size
≈ 5.5 cm long
Clinical note
Most of its surface is cartilage, so its blood supply is fragile and neck fractures risk avascular necrosis.
