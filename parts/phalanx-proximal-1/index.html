Proximal phalanx of the thumb

Phalanx proximalis digiti I manus

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Proximal phalanx of the thumb, a short long bone with a concave base and a pulley-shaped head.
Function
Forms a lever of the finger moved by the long flexor and extensor tendons.
Size
≈ 25 mm long
Clinical note
Each finger has three phalanges but the thumb only two (14 per hand).
