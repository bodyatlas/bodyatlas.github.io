Seminal vesicle

Glandula vesiculosa

ReproductiveOrgansPelvisLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

Shown on the male body.

What it is
Lobulated sac behind the bladder base, lateral to the ampulla of the vas deferens; it is really a single tube about 15 cm long folded on itself. Its duct joins the vas to form the ejaculatory duct.
Function
Secretes 60–70% of the semen volume: a thick, alkaline, fructose-rich fluid that fuels sperm.
Size
About 5 cm long and 1–2 cm wide.
Clinical note
Its secretion contains semenogelin, which makes semen clot briefly after ejaculation.
