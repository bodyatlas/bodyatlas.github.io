Tibial nerve

Nervus tibialis

NervousNervesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The larger division of the sciatic nerve. It runs straight down the middle of the popliteal fossa, passes under the fibrous arch of soleus, descends deep in the calf with the posterior tibial artery, curves behind the medial malleolus through the tarsal tunnel and divides into the medial and lateral plantar nerves of the sole.
Function
Motor to the calf muscles (pointing the foot and toes, inversion) and all the small muscles of the sole; sensation from the heel and the sole.
Size
About 4 mm thick; ~45 cm from the knee to the ankle
Clinical note
Tarsal tunnel syndrome - compression behind the medial malleolus - causes burning pain in the sole. Tibial nerve loss prevents standing on tiptoe and abolishes the ankle jerk.

Part of Sciatic nerve
