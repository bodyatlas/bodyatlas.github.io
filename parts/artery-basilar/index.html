Basilar artery

Arteria basilaris

CardiovascularBlood vesselsHead

View in 3D

What it is
Formed by the union of the two vertebral arteries at the lower border of the pons; it runs up the groove on the front of the pons and divides at its upper border into the two posterior cerebral arteries.
Function
Supplies the pons, cerebellum (via superior and anterior inferior cerebellar arteries), inner ear and, through its terminal branches, the occipital lobes.
Size
~3 cm long, ~3–4 mm diameter.
Clinical note
Basilar artery occlusion can cause "locked-in syndrome" — full consciousness with paralysis of all muscles except those moving the eyes.

Nearby cardiovascular structures Circle of Willis (ACA, MCA, PCA), External carotid artery, Internal carotid artery
