Superior mesenteric vein

Vena mesenterica superior

CardiovascularBlood vesselsAbdomen

View in 3D

What it is
Runs up in the root of the mesentery to the right of the superior mesenteric artery, passing in front of the third part of the duodenum and the uncinate process, to end behind the pancreatic neck by joining the splenic vein.
Function
Drains the small intestine, caecum, appendix, ascending and transverse colon into the portal vein.
Size
~1 cm diameter.
Clinical note
On CT a whirlpool of the SMV around the SMA is the sign of midgut volvulus.
