Vagus nerve root (CN X)

Radix nervi vagi

NervousOrgansHead

View in 3D

What it is
A fan of eight to ten rootlets leaving the postolivary sulcus of the medulla below the glossopharyngeal rootlets and uniting into one trunk. Shown as a short stub on both sides; the nerve continues in the peripheral nervous system.
Function
Parasympathetic supply to the heart, lungs and gut; motor to the palate, pharynx and larynx; sensation from the viscera.
Size
Stub about 1 cm; root 2.4 mm thick
Clinical note
Its motor nucleus (nucleus ambiguus) lies in the lateral medulla, so a lateral medullary (Wallenberg) stroke causes hoarseness and swallowing difficulty.
