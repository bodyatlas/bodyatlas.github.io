Glenoid labrum

Labrum glenoidale

SkeletalSkeletonThoraxLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Ring of fibrocartilage around the rim of the glenoid cavity, to which the long head of biceps is anchored above.
Function
Deepens the shallow socket by about 50% and stabilises the shoulder.
Size
Ring ≈ 30 × 22 mm, 4 mm thick
Clinical note
A SLAP tear (superior labrum anterior-to-posterior) is common in throwing athletes.
