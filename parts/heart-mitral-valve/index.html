Mitral valve

Valva atrioventricularis sinistra (valva mitralis)

CardiovascularOrgansThorax

View in 3D

What it is
The left atrioventricular valve, with a large anterior leaflet (continuous with the aortic valve) and a long, narrow posterior leaflet, tethered by chordae tendineae to two papillary muscles.
Function
Lets blood flow from the left atrium into the left ventricle and seals during systole so blood is ejected only into the aorta.
Size
Annulus ~3 cm diameter (circumference ~9–10 cm); opening area 4–6 cm².
Clinical note
Named for its resemblance to a bishop's mitre; mitral valve prolapse affects 2–3% of people, and rheumatic fever classically causes mitral stenosis.
