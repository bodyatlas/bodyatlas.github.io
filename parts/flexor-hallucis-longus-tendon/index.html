Flexor hallucis longus tendon

Tendo musculi flexoris hallucis longi

MuscularDeep musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Grooves the back of the talus, runs beneath the sustentaculum tali (which acts as its pulley) and along the sole between the sesamoids of the great toe to its distal phalanx.
Function
Flexes the great toe and supports the medial arch.
Size
About 20 cm long, 5 mm diameter.
Clinical note
Can be trapped behind the talus (posterior ankle impingement), producing pain and triggering of the big toe.
