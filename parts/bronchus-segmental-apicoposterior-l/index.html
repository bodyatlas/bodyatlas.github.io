Left apicoposterior segmental bronchus (B1+2)

Bronchus segmentalis apicoposterior

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Segmental (tertiary) bronchus B1+2, supplying the apicoposterior bronchopulmonary segment of the left upper lobe.
Function
Conducts air to one bronchopulmonary segment, a pyramidal unit of lung with its own bronchus and segmental artery.
Size
About 4 cm long and 4–6 mm in diameter; its wall holds irregular cartilage plates.
Clinical note
On the left the apical and posterior segmental bronchi share a common stem.
