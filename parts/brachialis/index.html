Brachialis

Musculus brachialis

MuscularDeep musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Broad flat muscle deep to biceps, from the lower half of the anterior humerus across the front of the elbow joint to the coronoid process and tuberosity of the ulna.
Function
The main flexor of the elbow, whatever the position of the forearm.
Size
About 15 cm long and 4–5 cm wide.
Clinical note
Bleeding into it after elbow injury can ossify (myositis ossificans), limiting movement.
