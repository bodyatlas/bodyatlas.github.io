What it is
The "anvil": the middle ossicle. Its body articulates with the malleus head, a short process points backwards into the attic, and a long process descends parallel to the malleus handle to end in the knob-like lenticular process that meets the stapes.
Function
Links malleus to stapes, adding lever action to the chain.
Size
About 7 mm long, 27 mg.
Clinical note
Its long process has the poorest blood supply of the chain and is the part most often eroded by chronic middle-ear disease, breaking the ossicular link.
