Extensor carpi radialis longus

Musculus extensor carpi radialis longus

What it is
Arises from the lower lateral supracondylar ridge of the humerus and runs along the radial side of the forearm to the dorsal base of the second metacarpal.
Function
Extends and radially deviates the wrist; keeps the wrist extended during a firm grip.
Size
About 25 cm long; fleshy in its upper half.
Clinical note
A frequent donor tendon for tendon transfers after nerve injuries.
