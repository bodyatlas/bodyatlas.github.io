Pisiform

Os pisiforme

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Pea-shaped sesamoid bone lying on the palmar face of the triquetrum within the flexor carpi ulnaris tendon.
Function
Proximal-row carpal: adapts its position as the wrist bends so the hand moves smoothly on the forearm.
Size
≈ 8 × 9 × 8 mm
Clinical note
Palpable at the base of the hypothenar eminence.
