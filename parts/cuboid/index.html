Cuboid

Os cuboideum

SkeletalSkeletonLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Cube-shaped bone on the lateral side of the midfoot, in front of the calcaneus, grooved underneath by the fibularis longus tendon.
Function
Forms part of the lateral longitudinal arch and supports the 4th and 5th metatarsals.
Size
≈ 2.5 × 2 × 2 cm
Clinical note
Cuboid syndrome (subluxation) is a cause of lateral foot pain in dancers.
