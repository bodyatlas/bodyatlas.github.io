Lateral semicircular canal

Canalis semicircularis lateralis

Sensory (eye & ear)OrgansHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The lateral (horizontal) semicircular canal, tilted about 30 degrees up at the front, bulges into the middle ear above the canal of the facial nerve; its ampulla lies at the anterior end next to the utricle.
Function
Senses turning the head from side to side (yaw).
Size
Radius about 3.2-4 mm; the shortest canal, about 15 mm.
Clinical note
Caloric testing irrigates the ear canal with warm or cool water to set up convection in this canal and provoke nystagmus.
