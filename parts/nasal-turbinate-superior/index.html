Superior nasal turbinate

Concha nasalis superior

What it is
Superior nasal concha (turbinate): a scroll of thin bone covered by thick, vascular mucosa projecting from the lateral wall of the nasal cavity.
Function
Increases the mucosal surface and creates turbulent airflow so inhaled air is warmed, humidified and filtered.
Size
The smallest turbinate, part of the ethmoid, about 2 cm long; the posterior ethmoid cells drain beneath it and the sphenoid sinus opens above and behind it.
Clinical note
Olfactory epithelium covers its upper surface, next to the cribriform plate.
