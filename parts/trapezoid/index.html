Trapezoid

Os trapezoideum

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Small wedge-shaped distal-row bone at the base of the index metacarpal.
Function
Distal-row carpal: forms a rigid unit with the metacarpals and transmits grip forces to the forearm.
Size
≈ 10 × 12 × 12 mm
Clinical note
The least commonly fractured carpal bone.

Nearby skeletal structures Capitate, First metacarpal, Hamate, Lunate, Pisiform, Scaphoid, Second metacarpal, Third metacarpal, Trapezium, Triquetrum
