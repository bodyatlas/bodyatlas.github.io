Tricuspid valve

Valva atrioventricularis dextra (valva tricuspidalis)

CardiovascularOrgansThorax

View in 3D

What it is
The right atrioventricular valve, with anterior, posterior and septal leaflets anchored by chordae tendineae to the papillary muscles of the right ventricle.
Function
Allows blood to pass from the right atrium to the right ventricle and closes in systole to prevent regurgitation into the right atrium and veins.
Size
Largest valve: annulus ~3.5–4 cm diameter (circumference 11–13 cm); opening area 5–8 cm².
Clinical note
Closure of the mitral and tricuspid valves makes the first heart sound (S1); tricuspid endocarditis is typical in people who inject drugs.
