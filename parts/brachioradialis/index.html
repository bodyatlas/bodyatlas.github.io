Brachioradialis

Musculus brachioradialis

MuscularSuperficial musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Superficial muscle along the radial (thumb) border of the forearm, from the upper lateral supracondylar ridge of the humerus to the base of the radial styloid process.
Function
Flexes the elbow, most strongly with the forearm half-pronated (thumb up, as when drinking).
Size
About 25 cm long; belly 3–4 cm wide.
Clinical note
Although it lies in the extensor compartment and is supplied by the radial nerve, it flexes the elbow; its reflex tests C6.
