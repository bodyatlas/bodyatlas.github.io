What it is
Schematic enlargement of one juxtamedullary nephron, the kidney's filtering unit: the renal corpuscle and convoluted tubules sit in the cortex (above the line), the loop of Henle and collecting duct run down into the medulla (below it).
Function
Each nephron filters blood and then reabsorbs or secretes water and solutes along its tubule to produce urine.
Size
About 1 million per kidney; a nephron tubule is 3–5 cm long but only ~50 µm wide (not drawn to one scale).
Clinical note
About 85% of nephrons are cortical with short loops; the 15% juxtamedullary nephrons with long loops make concentrated urine possible.
