Internal intercostal muscles

Musculi intercostales interni

MuscularDeep musclesWhole bodyLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Eleven thin sheets deep to the external intercostals with fibres at right angles to them (down and backwards), from the sternum and costal cartilages back to the angles of the ribs, where the internal intercostal membrane takes over.
Function
Pull the ribs down in forced expiration; their front (interchondral) parts help raise the ribs in inspiration.
Size
Each space ~1.5–2 cm tall; sheets 2–3 mm thick.
Clinical note
The intercostal neurovascular bundle runs in the costal groove between this layer and the innermost intercostals.
