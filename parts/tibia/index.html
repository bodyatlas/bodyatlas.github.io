What it is
The shin bone: a broad upper end with two flat condyles (the tibial plateau) and the tibial tuberosity, a triangular shaft with a sharp anterior border just under the skin, and a lower end forming the medial malleolus.
Function
Carries almost all the body weight from knee to ankle and forms the roof and inner wall of the ankle joint.
Size
≈ 41 cm long; plateau ≈ 7.5 cm wide
Clinical note
Because its front lies directly under the skin, the tibia is the long bone most often broken open (compound fracture).
