What it is
The medial (little-finger-side) forearm bone: a large hooked upper end (olecranon and coronoid process around the trochlear notch) that grips the humeral trochlea, tapering to a small head and styloid process at the wrist.
Function
Forms the hinge of the elbow and the stable axis around which the radius rotates.
Size
≈ 25 cm long
Clinical note
The olecranon is the point of the elbow; it is fractured by falls directly onto the elbow.
