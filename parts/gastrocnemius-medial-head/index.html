Gastrocnemius (medial head)

Musculus gastrocnemius, caput mediale

What it is
The larger head of gastrocnemius, arising from the back of the medial femoral condyle. It forms the prominent inner bulge of the calf, extends lower than the lateral head and blends into the calcaneal (Achilles) tendon.
Function
Plantarflexes the ankle (push-off, standing on tiptoe) and flexes the knee.
Size
Belly about 20 cm long, 6-7 cm wide.
Clinical note
"Tennis leg" is a tear of its musculotendinous junction on the inner calf, typically in middle-aged recreational athletes.
