Leg subcutaneous fat

Tela subcutanea membri inferioris

Integumentary (skin)Fat & fasciaLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The fatty layer of the thigh and leg above the fascia lata and crural fascia, carrying the great and small saphenous veins and cutaneous nerves.
Function
Insulation, energy storage and a gliding plane over the muscles.
Size
5–25 mm on the thigh (thicker in women), a few mm over the shin
Clinical note
The great saphenous vein in this layer is the vessel most often harvested for coronary bypass grafts.
