Cricoid cartilage

Cartilago cricoidea

RespiratoryOrgansNeck

View in 3D

What it is
The only complete ring of cartilage in the airway, shaped like a signet ring: a narrow arch in front and a tall lamina behind.
Function
Forms the base of the larynx, carrying the arytenoid and thyroid cartilages on synovial joints, and holds the airway open at the laryngo-tracheal junction.
Size
About 2.5 cm across; arch 5–7 mm high, lamina 2–3 cm high; at the level of C6.
Clinical note
An emergency airway (cricothyrotomy) is made through the cricothyroid membrane just above its arch; it is the narrowest point of a young child's airway.
