Masseter

Musculus masseter

What it is
Thick quadrilateral muscle on the side of the jaw, running from the lower border of the zygomatic arch to the lateral surface of the ramus and angle of the mandible.
Function
Elevates the mandible, closing the jaw with great force; its superficial part also helps protrude it.
Size
About 5 cm tall, 3 cm wide and up to 1.5 cm thick.
Clinical note
Enlarges with habitual clenching or bruxism (masseteric hypertrophy), which is sometimes treated with botulinum toxin.
