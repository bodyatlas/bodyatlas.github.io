What it is
Thin muscle hidden between tibialis anterior and extensor digitorum longus, arising from the middle of the anterior fibula and the interosseous membrane.
Function
Extends the great toe and helps dorsiflex the ankle.
Size
Belly about 15 cm long.
Clinical note
Testing the strength of great-toe extension is the classic check of the L5 nerve root.
