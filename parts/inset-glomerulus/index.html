What it is
Tuft of looping capillaries fed by the wider afferent arteriole and drained by the narrower efferent arteriole, which keeps the pressure inside high.
Function
Filters plasma through fenestrated endothelium, a basement membrane and podocyte filtration slits; together the kidneys make about 180 L of filtrate a day (GFR ~125 mL/min).
Size
About 0.2 mm across.
Clinical note
Damage to the filter (diabetic nephropathy, glomerulonephritis) lets protein and blood leak into the urine.
