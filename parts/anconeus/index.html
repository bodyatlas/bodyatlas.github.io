Anconeus

Musculus anconeus

MuscularDeep musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Small triangular muscle behind the elbow, from the back of the lateral epicondyle to the lateral surface of the olecranon and upper posterior ulna.
Function
Assists triceps in extending the elbow and steadies the ulna during pronation.
Size
About 4 cm long.
Clinical note
Often regarded as a continuation of triceps; the Kocher approach to the radial head passes between it and extensor carpi ulnaris.
