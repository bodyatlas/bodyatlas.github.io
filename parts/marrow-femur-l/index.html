Bone marrow of left femur

Medulla ossium

SkeletalSkeletonLegLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Soft tissue filling the medullary cavity of the femoral shaft, revealed by peeling away the compact bone; in adults the shaft holds mostly fatty yellow marrow.
Function
Red marrow (in the ends of long bones, pelvis, sternum and vertebrae) makes about 500 billion blood cells a day; yellow marrow stores fat and can convert back when needed.
Size
Medullary cavity ≈ 15 mm across, ≈ 25 cm long
Clinical note
A large femoral fracture can release marrow fat into the bloodstream (fat embolism syndrome).
