Hypodermis (magnified skin block)

Tela subcutanea (hypodermis)

Integumentary (skin)SkinLeg

View in 3D

What it is
The subcutaneous layer below the dermis: lobules of fat cells (adipocytes) separated by thin fibrous septa that tether the skin to the deep fascia.
Function
Insulates, stores energy, cushions and lets the skin move over deeper structures.
Size
Real thickness a few mm to several cm depending on site and body fat; lobules ~1 cm, adipocytes ~0.1 mm
Clinical note
Insulin and many vaccines are injected into this layer (subcutaneous injection) because its blood supply gives slow, steady absorption.
