Palmaris longus

Musculus palmaris longus

MuscularSuperficial musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Small spindle-shaped muscle from the medial epicondyle with a long slender tendon passing superficial to the flexor retinaculum into the palmar aponeurosis.
Function
Weakly flexes the wrist and tenses the palmar aponeurosis.
Size
Belly ~6 cm, tendon ~15 cm.
Clinical note
Absent in about 15% of people; its tendon is a favourite graft for tendon and ligament reconstruction.
