Tibialis posterior tendon

Tendo musculi tibialis posterioris

MuscularDeep musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Tendon of tibialis posterior: it grooves the back of the medial malleolus, then runs forward under the spring ligament to the tuberosity of the navicular, with slips to the cuneiforms, cuboid and bases of metatarsals 2-4.
Function
Pulls the foot into inversion and supports the arch from below.
Size
About 15 cm long, 6 mm diameter.
Clinical note
Degeneration where it rounds the medial malleolus (a zone of poor blood supply) leads to progressive collapse of the arch.
