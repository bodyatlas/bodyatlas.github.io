Plantar fascia

Aponeurosis plantaris

What it is
The plantar aponeurosis: a thick, pearly fibrous sheet under the sole, from the medial tubercle of the calcaneus fanning out into five slips that attach to the toes via the plantar plates and flexor sheaths.
Function
Ties the heel to the toes like a bowstring, supporting the longitudinal arch; it tightens when the toes extend at push-off (windlass mechanism).
Size
About 20 cm long; 2-4 mm thick at the heel.
Clinical note
Plantar fasciitis - heel pain worst with the first steps in the morning - is the commonest cause of heel pain in adults.
