Pronator teres

Musculus pronator teres

MuscularDeep musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Two-headed muscle from the medial epicondyle (humeral head) and coronoid process (ulnar head) crossing the front of the forearm obliquely to the middle of the lateral surface of the radius.
Function
Pronates the forearm and assists elbow flexion.
Size
About 13 cm long.
Clinical note
The median nerve passes between its two heads, where it can be compressed (pronator syndrome).
