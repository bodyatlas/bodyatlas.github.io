Skin of the penis

Cutis penis

ReproductiveSkinPelvis

View in 3D

Shown on the male body.

What it is
Thin, hairless, loosely attached skin over the shaft that slides freely and folds over the glans as the prepuce (foreskin).
Function
Covers the erectile bodies while allowing large changes in size.
Size
Flaccid penis about 9 cm long and 9–10 cm around (average).
Clinical note
Lymph from the penile skin drains to the superficial inguinal nodes, whereas the glans also drains to deep nodes.
