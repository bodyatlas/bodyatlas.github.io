Iliocostalis

Musculus iliocostalis

MuscularDeep musclesWhole bodyLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Lateral column of the erector spinae, rising from the sacrum and iliac crest (common erector tendon) along the angles of the ribs to the transverse processes of C4–C6 (lumborum, thoracis and cervicis parts).
Function
Extends the spine and bends it to its own side; the lumbar part helps depress the lower ribs.
Size
About 60 cm long; up to 3 cm wide in the loin.
Clinical note
Spasm of the lumbar erector spinae is the usual cause of acute "lumbago".
