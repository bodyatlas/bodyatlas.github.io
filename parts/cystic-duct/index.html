Cystic duct

Ductus cysticus

DigestiveOrgansAbdomen

View in 3D

What it is
Short, tortuous duct from the neck of the gallbladder to the common hepatic duct; its lining forms a spiral fold (valve of Heister).
Function
Lets bile flow into the gallbladder for storage and out again when it contracts.
Size
About 3-4 cm long and 2-3 mm wide.
Clinical note
A stone impacted here causes biliary colic or acute cholecystitis.

Part of Gallbladder
