Optic tract root (CN II)

Radix tractus optici

NervousOrgansHead

View in 3D

What it is
The start of each optic tract at the back corner of the optic chiasm, where fibres from both eyes regroup before sweeping round the cerebral peduncle toward the lateral geniculate body. Shown as a short stub on both sides; the nerve continues in the peripheral nervous system.
Function
Carries the opposite half of the visual field from both eyes.
Size
Stub about 1 cm; root 3.8 mm thick
Clinical note
A pituitary tumour pressing up on the chiasm from below classically causes bitemporal hemianopia.
