Supinator

Musculus supinator

MuscularDeep musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Deep curved sheet wrapping around the upper third of the radius, from the lateral epicondyle, radial collateral and annular ligaments and the supinator crest of the ulna.
Function
Supinates the forearm (turns the palm up), especially with the elbow straight.
Size
About 6 cm long.
Clinical note
The deep branch of the radial nerve passes through it beneath the arcade of Frohse, a site of nerve entrapment.
