Calcaneus

What it is
The heel bone, largest of the foot: a long block with a posterior tuberosity, a medial shelf (sustentaculum tali) supporting the talus, and a front facet for the cuboid.
Function
Forms the heel, bears the body weight on landing and anchors the Achilles tendon.
Size
≈ 7.5–8 cm long, 4 cm tall
Clinical note
Usually fractured by falls from a height landing on the heels.
