Biceps femoris (short head)

Musculus biceps femoris, caput breve

MuscularDeep musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Arises from the lateral lip of the linea aspera and lateral supracondylar line of the femur, deep to the long head, and joins it in the common tendon to the fibular head.
Function
Flexes the knee and laterally rotates the leg.
Size
About 15-20 cm long.
Clinical note
The only part of the hamstrings that does not cross the hip; it is supplied by the common fibular division of the sciatic nerve.
