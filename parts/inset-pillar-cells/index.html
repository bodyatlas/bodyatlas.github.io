Pillar cells (magnified)

Cellulae pilares

Sensory (eye & ear)OrgansWhole body

View in 3D

What it is
Inner and outer pillar cells lean against each other to form the triangular tunnel of Corti between the inner and outer hair cells; with Deiters' cells they make up the stiff scaffold of the organ.
Function
Give the organ of Corti its rigidity and form the reticular lamina at its surface through which the stereocilia project.
Size
Real height about 50 micrometres.
Clinical note
The tunnel is crossed by thin efferent nerve fibres running from the brainstem to the outer hair cells.
