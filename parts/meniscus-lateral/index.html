Lateral meniscus

Meniscus lateralis

SkeletalSkeletonLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Almost circular (O-shaped) fibrocartilage ring on the lateral tibial plateau.
Function
Distributes load across the lateral compartment and aids knee stability.
Size
≈ 3.5 cm across, 10 mm wide
Clinical note
It is more mobile than the medial meniscus, which partly protects it from tearing.

Nearby skeletal structures Bone marrow of left femur, Femoral condyle cartilage, Femoral head cartilage, Femur, Fibula, Medial meniscus, Patella, Patellar cartilage, Talus, Tibia
