Left anteromedial basal segmental bronchus (B7+8)

Bronchus segmentalis anteromedial basal

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Segmental (tertiary) bronchus B7+8, supplying the anteromedial basal bronchopulmonary segment of the left lower lobe.
Function
Conducts air to one bronchopulmonary segment, a pyramidal unit of lung with its own bronchus and segmental artery.
Size
About 2 cm long and 4–6 mm in diameter; its wall holds irregular cartilage plates.
Clinical note
On the left the medial and anterior basal bronchi usually share a common stem.
