What it is
Sphincter of the mouth made of fibres encircling the lips; many are continuations of buccinator and the other lip muscles converging at the modioli, with small incisive slips to the maxilla and mandible.
Function
Closes, purses and protrudes the lips (whistling, kissing) and keeps food in the mouth while chewing.
Size
Ring about 6 cm wide and 3.5 cm tall around the oral fissure.
Clinical note
Its fibres must be re-joined precisely in cleft-lip repair for normal lip movement and speech.
