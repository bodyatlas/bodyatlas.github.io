Distal convoluted tubule (magnified)

Tubulus contortus distalis

UrinaryOrgansWhole body

View in 3D

What it is
Short coiled tubule in the cortex that begins at the macula densa, where the tubule touches its own glomerulus to form the juxtaglomerular apparatus, and drains via a connecting tubule into a collecting duct.
Function
Fine-tunes sodium, chloride and calcium reabsorption (thiazide-sensitive NCC cotransporter; calcium under parathyroid hormone control).
Size
About 5 mm long.
Clinical note
The macula densa senses tubular salt and signals the neighbouring cells to release renin when it falls.
