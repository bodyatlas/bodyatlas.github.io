Coccyx

Os coccygis

SkeletalSkeletonPelvis

View in 3D

What it is
The tailbone: three to five small rudimentary vertebrae fused into a tapering, forward-curving triangle.
Function
Anchors the gluteus maximus, the anococcygeal ligament and pelvic-floor muscles.
Size
≈ 40 mm long
Clinical note
It is the vestigial remnant of a tail; a fall on the buttocks can bruise or fracture it (coccydynia).
