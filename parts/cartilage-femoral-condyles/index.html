Femoral condyle cartilage

Cartilago articularis condylorum femoris

SkeletalSkeletonLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Hyaline cartilage capping the trochlear groove and both femoral condyles.
Function
Lets the femur roll and glide on the tibia and patella through 140° of knee flexion.
Size
≈ 2–3 mm thick
Clinical note
Cartilage has no blood supply, so defects heal poorly; osteoarthritis of the knee is extremely common.
