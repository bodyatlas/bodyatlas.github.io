Gemelli and quadratus femoris

Musculi gemelli superior et inferior; musculus quadratus femoris

MuscularDeep musclesPelvisLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Three small deep rotators behind the hip: the superior gemellus (from the ischial spine) and inferior gemellus (from the ischial tuberosity) run alongside the obturator internus tendon to the greater trochanter; below them quadratus femoris, a flat rectangle, passes from the ischial tuberosity to the quadrate tubercle on the intertrochanteric crest.
Function
Laterally rotate the hip and hold the femoral head in its socket.
Size
Gemelli about 6-8 cm long and 1 cm thick; quadratus femoris about 7 x 4 cm.
Clinical note
With piriformis and the obturators they form the "short external rotators" that are detached and repaired in the posterior approach to hip replacement.
