Renal cortex (right kidney, cut)

Cortex renalis

UrinaryOrgansAbdomenRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Outer granular zone of the kidney, about 1 cm thick, containing the glomeruli and convoluted tubules; it dips between the pyramids as the renal columns (of Bertin).
Function
Site of blood filtration in the renal corpuscles and of most tubular reabsorption; receives about 90% of renal blood flow.
Size
About 1 cm thick beneath the capsule.
Clinical note
Shown as the posterior half of a coronal section so the medulla and collecting system are visible.

Part of Kidney
