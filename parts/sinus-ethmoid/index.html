Ethmoid air cells

Cellulae ethmoidales

RespiratoryOrgansHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
A honeycomb of 3–18 small air cells in the ethmoid labyrinth, between the upper nasal cavity and the orbit, grouped as anterior, middle and posterior cells.
Function
Lighten the skull and drain into the middle and superior meatuses.
Size
Individual cells 2–10 mm; the labyrinth is ~4–5 cm long and 1–2.5 cm wide.
Clinical note
The lateral wall (lamina papyracea) is paper-thin, so ethmoid sinusitis in children can spread into the orbit (orbital cellulitis).
