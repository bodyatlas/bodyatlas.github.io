Rhomboid minor

Musculus rhomboideus minor

MuscularDeep musclesThoraxLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Small band above rhomboid major, from the lower ligamentum nuchae and the spinous processes of C7 and T1 to the medial border of the scapula at the root of its spine.
Function
Retracts and elevates the scapula together with rhomboid major.
Size
About 2 cm wide and 8 cm long.
Clinical note
Often fused with rhomboid major; both are supplied by the dorsal scapular nerve (C5).
