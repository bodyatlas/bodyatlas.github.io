Lower lip

Labium inferius oris

Integumentary (skin)SkinHead

View in 3D

What it is
The lower lip vermilion, fuller than the upper lip, resting on the lower incisors. The red colour comes from blood in capillaries seen through a thin, non-keratinised, hair-less epithelium.
Function
Closes the mouth, seals around food and drink, shapes speech sounds and is one of the most touch-sensitive areas of the body.
Size
Vermilion ~5 cm wide, ~10 mm high
Clinical note
The lips lack sweat and sebaceous glands and so dry and crack easily; the vermilion border must be realigned exactly when repairing a cut lip or cleft lip.
