Right medial segmental bronchus (B5)

Bronchus segmentalis medial

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Segmental (tertiary) bronchus B5, supplying the medial bronchopulmonary segment of the right middle lobe.
Function
Conducts air to one bronchopulmonary segment, a pyramidal unit of lung with its own bronchus and segmental artery.
Size
About 4 cm long and 4–6 mm in diameter; its wall holds irregular cartilage plates.
Clinical note
Because each segment has its own airway and blood supply, a diseased segment can be removed on its own (segmentectomy).
