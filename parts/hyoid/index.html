Hyoid bone

Os hyoideum

SkeletalSkeletonNeck

View in 3D

What it is
U-shaped bone in the front of the neck at the level of C3, with a body and greater and lesser horns; it touches no other bone and hangs from muscles and ligaments.
Function
Anchors the tongue and the muscles that raise and lower the larynx during swallowing and speech.
Size
≈ 4 cm across, body 2.5 cm wide
Clinical note
A fractured hyoid is a classic forensic sign of strangulation.
