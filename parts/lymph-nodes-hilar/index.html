Hilar lymph nodes

Nodi lymphoidei bronchopulmonales

Lymphatic & immuneLymphaticsThoraxLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Bronchopulmonary nodes packed around the main bronchus and pulmonary vessels at the root (hilum) of each lung, where the airway enters it. Each node is a bean-shaped filter 5-15 mm long with a fibrous capsule, an outer cortex of B-cell follicles, a T-cell paracortex and an inner medulla. Several afferent lymphatics enter its convex surface; one or two efferents leave at the hilum.
Function
First filter for lymph draining from the lung tissue and airways, before it passes to the tracheobronchial and mediastinal nodes.
Size
5-10 nodes of 5-15 mm per side
Clinical note
In adult city-dwellers and smokers these nodes are black with inhaled carbon (anthracosis). Enlargement is seen in tuberculosis, sarcoidosis and lung cancer.
