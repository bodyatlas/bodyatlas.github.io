Central retinal vein

Vena centralis retinae

Sensory (eye & ear)OrgansHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Formed at the optic disc by four retinal venous branches that run beside the arteries; it leaves the optic nerve with the artery and drains into the superior ophthalmic vein or cavernous sinus.
Function
Drains blood from the inner retina.
Size
About 1.5 times the width of the matching artery (artery:vein ratio ~2:3).
Clinical note
A central retinal vein occlusion gives a "blood-and-thunder" fundus with widespread haemorrhages; loss of spontaneous venous pulsation at the disc can hint at raised intracranial pressure.
