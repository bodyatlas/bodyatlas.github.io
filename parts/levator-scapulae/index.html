Levator scapulae

Musculus levator scapulae

MuscularDeep musclesWhole bodyLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Strap muscle from the transverse processes of C1–C4 descending along the side and back of the neck to the superior angle and upper medial border of the scapula.
Function
Elevates the scapula and rotates it so the glenoid tilts downwards; bends the neck to its side.
Size
About 15 cm long and 2 cm wide.
Clinical note
A frequent source of "stiff neck" and trigger-point pain at the angle between neck and shoulder.
