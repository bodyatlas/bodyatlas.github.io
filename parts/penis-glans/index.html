Glans penis

ReproductiveOrgansPelvis

View in 3D

Shown on the male body.

What it is
Cone-shaped cap formed by the expanded end of the corpus spongiosum, bordered by the raised corona and bearing the slit-like external urethral meatus.
Function
Densely innervated sensory tip of the penis that also directs the urinary stream.
Size
About 2 cm long and 3 cm across the corona.
Clinical note
Covered by the prepuce (foreskin) unless circumcised; in hypospadias the meatus opens on its underside.
