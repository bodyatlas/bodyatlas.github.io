Tracheal cartilages (C-rings)

Cartilagines tracheales

RespiratoryOrgansWhole body

View in 3D

What it is
Eighteen horseshoe-shaped rings of hyaline cartilage embedded in the tracheal wall, open at the back where the trachea touches the oesophagus.
Function
Keep the airway from collapsing under the negative pressure of inspiration while leaving the posterior wall flexible.
Size
Each ring is about 4 mm high and 1 mm thick, joined to its neighbours by fibrous annular ligaments.
Clinical note
The rings often calcify with age; congenital complete "O-rings" are a cause of tracheal stenosis in infants.
