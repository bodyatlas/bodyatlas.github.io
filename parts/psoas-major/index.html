Psoas major

Musculus psoas major

MuscularDeep musclesWhole bodyLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Long spindle-shaped muscle beside the lumbar spine, from the sides of the T12–L5 vertebral bodies, their discs and transverse processes, running down along the pelvic brim and under the inguinal ligament to join iliacus on the lesser trochanter of the femur.
Function
The main flexor of the hip; also flexes the trunk on the thigh (sitting up) and bends the lumbar spine sideways.
Size
About 30 cm long and up to 5 cm thick at the lower lumbar spine.
Clinical note
A spinal abscess can track down its sheath to the groin; pain on hip extension ("psoas sign") suggests an inflamed retrocaecal appendix.
