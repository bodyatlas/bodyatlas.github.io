Left common carotid artery

Arteria carotis communis sinistra

CardiovascularBlood vesselsWhole bodyLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Ascends in the carotid sheath beside the trachea and larynx, with the internal jugular vein lateral and the vagus nerve behind, dividing at the upper border of the thyroid cartilage (C3–C4). The left arises directly from the aortic arch, so it has a thoracic part.
Function
Carries blood to the head, neck and brain.
Size
~7–8 mm diameter; ~12 cm long.
Clinical note
The carotid pulse is felt at the front border of sternocleidomastoid; the carotid sinus at the bifurcation senses blood pressure and atherosclerotic plaque there is a major cause of stroke.
