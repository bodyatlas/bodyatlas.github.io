Left lung, lower lobe

Lobus inferior pulmonis sinistri

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
The left lower lobe lies below and behind the oblique fissure and rests on the left dome of the diaphragm; its medial surface is grooved by the descending aorta.
Function
Gas exchange; ventilated by the left lower lobe bronchus (superior, anteromedial basal, lateral basal and posterior basal segments).
Size
About 23% of total lung volume; the left lung as a whole is ~10% smaller than the right because of the heart.
Clinical note
Lower-lobe pneumonia irritating the diaphragmatic pleura can refer pain to the shoulder tip (phrenic nerve, C3–C5) or the upper abdomen.
