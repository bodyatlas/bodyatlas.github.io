Central tendon of the diaphragm

Centrum tendineum

MuscularDeep musclesThorax

View in 3D

What it is
Thin, strong trefoil-shaped aponeurosis at the top of the diaphragm with anterior, right and left leaflets; the fibrous pericardium is fused to its upper surface and the inferior vena cava passes through it.
Function
Gives all the diaphragm's muscle fibres a common insertion, so their contraction pulls the dome down.
Size
About 10 cm across the leaflets and ~1 mm thick.
Clinical note
Because the vena caval opening lies in the tendon, inspiration stretches it open, helping blood return to the heart.

Nearby muscular structures Aortic hiatus (median arcuate ligament), Caval opening of the diaphragm, Infraspinatus, Oesophageal hiatus, Pectoralis major, Pectoralis minor, Rhomboid major, Serratus anterior, Subscapularis, Teres major
