Teres minor

Musculus teres minor

MuscularDeep musclesThoraxLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Narrow rotator-cuff muscle from the upper two-thirds of the lateral border of the scapula to the lowest facet of the greater tubercle of the humerus.
Function
Laterally rotates the arm and holds the humeral head in place.
Size
About 10 cm long and 2 cm wide.
Clinical note
Unlike the other posterior cuff muscles it is supplied by the axillary nerve.
