Zygomaticus major

Musculus zygomaticus major

What it is
Slender strap from the lateral surface of the zygomatic bone running down and forwards to the modiolus at the corner of the mouth.
Function
Draws the angle of the mouth upwards and outwards — the principal smiling muscle.
Size
About 6 cm long and 1 cm wide.
Clinical note
A bifid zygomaticus major is thought to cause cheek dimples; a genuine (Duchenne) smile combines it with orbicularis oculi.
