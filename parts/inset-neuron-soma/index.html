Neuron cell body (soma) - magnified

Soma (pericaryon) neuronis

NervousNervesWhole body

View in 3D

What it is
Magnified model (about 1000x) of a multipolar neuron such as a spinal motor neuron. The cell body holds the nucleus, stacks of rough endoplasmic reticulum (Nissl bodies) and the machinery that makes the proteins for the whole cell.
Function
Keeps the entire neuron alive and integrates the signals arriving on its dendrites before firing an impulse from the axon hillock.
Size
Real soma 20-100 micrometres across; shown ~16 mm
Clinical note
Neurons do not divide, so a lost cell body is not replaced; motor neurone disease (ALS) destroys exactly these cells.

Contains Axon, Dendrites, Myelin sheath, Neuron nucleus, Nodes of Ranvier, Postsynaptic dendrite of the next neuron, Synapse (axon terminals and boutons)
