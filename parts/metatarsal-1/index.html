First metatarsal

Os metatarsi I

SkeletalSkeletonLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Long bone of the forefoot behind the big toe (hallux), with a wedge-shaped base, slender shaft and rounded head at the ball of the foot.
Function
Short and thick, it carries about a third of forefoot load during push-off.
Size
≈ 65 mm long
Clinical note
Bunions (hallux valgus) develop at the first metatarsal head.
