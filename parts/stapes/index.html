Stapes

What it is
The "stirrup": the innermost and smallest ossicle. Its head receives the incus; two slender crura (anterior and posterior) run down to an oval footplate that sits in the oval window, held by the annular ligament.
Function
Pistons in the oval window, driving pressure waves into the perilymph of the inner ear.
Size
About 3.3 mm long; footplate 3 x 1.4 mm; weight 3 mg, the smallest bone in the body.
Clinical note
In otosclerosis new bone fixes the footplate, causing conductive hearing loss; a stapedectomy replaces it with a tiny piston prosthesis.
