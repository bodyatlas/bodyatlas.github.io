Gastrocnemius (lateral head)

Musculus gastrocnemius, caput laterale

What it is
The smaller, shorter head of gastrocnemius, arising from the lateral surface of the lateral femoral condyle and joining the medial head in the calcaneal tendon.
Function
Plantarflexes the ankle and flexes the knee.
Size
Belly about 15-18 cm long, 4-5 cm wide.
Clinical note
A small sesamoid bone, the fabella, is found in its tendon in a minority of people and can be mistaken for a loose body on knee X-rays.
