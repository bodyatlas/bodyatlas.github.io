Lateral cuneiform

Os cuneiforme laterale

SkeletalSkeletonLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Wedge-shaped cuneiform between the intermediate cuneiform and the cuboid, at the base of the third metatarsal.
Function
Keystone of the transverse arch of the foot.
Size
≈ 2 × 2 × 1.8 cm
Clinical note
Together the cuneiforms and cuboid make the foot a flexible, arched lever.
