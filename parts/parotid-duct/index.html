Parotid duct

Ductus parotideus (Stensen)

Endocrine & glandsGlandsHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The parotid (Stensen's) duct leaves the front edge of the gland, runs forward over the masseter muscle a finger-breadth below the cheekbone, turns sharply inward through the buccinator muscle and opens inside the cheek opposite the upper second molar.
Function
Carries parotid saliva into the mouth; the buccinator acts as a valve so the duct is not inflated when you blow.
Size
About 5 cm long, 3 mm in diameter
Clinical note
A small papilla marks its opening inside the cheek — press the gland and saliva squirts from it. Stones are rarer here than in the submandibular duct because parotid saliva is thin.
