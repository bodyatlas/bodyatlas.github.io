Descending colon

Colon descendens

DigestiveOrgansAbdomen

View in 3D

What it is
Runs down the left flank from the splenic flexure to the left iliac fossa; retroperitoneal and narrower than the right colon.
Function
Stores and propels increasingly solid faeces toward the sigmoid colon.
Size
About 25 cm long and about 4 cm wide.
Clinical note
Diverticula - pouches pushed out where vessels pierce the wall - are common here and in the sigmoid in people on low-fibre diets.
