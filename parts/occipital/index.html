Occipital bone

Os occipitale

SkeletalSkeletonHead

View in 3D

What it is
The back and base of the skull: a curved squama with the external occipital protuberance, cupping the cerebellum, and a base pierced by the foramen magnum with the occipital condyles on either side.
Function
Protects the occipital lobes and cerebellum and articulates with the atlas to carry the head.
Size
Foramen magnum ≈ 35 × 30 mm at y 1.578
Clinical note
The spinal cord becomes the medulla oblongata as it passes up through the foramen magnum.
