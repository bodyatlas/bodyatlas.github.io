Accessory nerve root (CN XI)

Radix nervi accessorii

NervousOrgansHead

View in 3D

What it is
Its cranial rootlets leave the lower medulla behind the olive, where the spinal root, which climbs from the upper cervical cord through the foramen magnum, joins them. Shown as a short stub on both sides; the nerve continues in the peripheral nervous system.
Function
Motor to sternocleidomastoid and trapezius (spinal part); the cranial part joins the vagus for the larynx.
Size
Stub about 1 cm; root 1.6 mm thick
Clinical note
Often injured in posterior-triangle neck surgery, causing a drooping shoulder.
