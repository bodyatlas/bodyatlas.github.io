Medial rectus

Musculus rectus medialis

Sensory (eye & ear)Deep musclesHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
A flat strap muscle arising from the common tendinous ring at the orbit apex and running forward along the orbital wall to insert by a thin, broad, white tendon on the sclera in front of the equator.
Function
Rotates the eye inward (adduction), towards the nose.
Size
The largest and strongest rectus, ~40 mm long; its tendon inserts ~5.5 mm from the limbus.
Clinical note
Supplied by the oculomotor nerve (CN III); weakness causes an outward-drifting eye. Converging both medial recti lets us look at near objects.
