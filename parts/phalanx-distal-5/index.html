Distal phalanx of the little finger

Phalanx distalis digiti V manus

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Distal phalanx of the little finger, ending in a rough tuft that supports the fingertip pulp and nail.
Function
Forms a lever of the finger moved by the long flexor and extensor tendons.
Size
≈ 13 mm long
Clinical note
Crush injuries of the fingertip commonly fracture the distal tuft.
