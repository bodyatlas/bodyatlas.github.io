Buccinator

Musculus buccinator

MuscularDeep musclesHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Thin quadrilateral muscle forming the wall of the cheek, from the alveolar processes of the maxilla and mandible opposite the molars and the pterygomandibular raphe, running forwards into the modiolus and orbicularis oris.
Function
Presses the cheek against the teeth to keep food between the molars and expels air from the mouth (blowing, playing a trumpet).
Size
About 5 cm long, 2–3 cm tall, 2–3 mm thick.
Clinical note
Pierced by the parotid duct opposite the upper second molar; although named after the trumpeter, it is supplied by the facial nerve.

Nearby muscular structures Frontalis, Masseter, Orbicularis oculi, Orbicularis oris, Temporalis, Zygomaticus major
