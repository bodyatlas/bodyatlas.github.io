Quadriceps tendon

Tendo musculi quadricipitis femoris

MuscularSuperficial musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Common tendon of the four quadriceps heads, attaching to the base (upper border) of the patella; its superficial fibres continue over the patella into the patellar ligament.
Function
Transmits the pull of the quadriceps across the knee via the patella, which acts as a pulley that increases its leverage.
Size
About 3 cm wide, 7-8 mm thick and 4-5 cm long above the patella.
Clinical note
Ruptures mainly in people over 40, often with diabetes, kidney disease or steroid use; the patient cannot straighten the knee against gravity.
