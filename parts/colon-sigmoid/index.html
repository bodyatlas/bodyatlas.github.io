Sigmoid colon

Colon sigmoideum

DigestiveOrgansPelvis

View in 3D

What it is
S-shaped loop on its own mesentery (sigmoid mesocolon) from the pelvic brim to the rectum in front of the third sacral vertebra.
Function
Stores faeces until defecation.
Size
About 40 cm long (very variable) and 3-4 cm wide.
Clinical note
Its long mobile mesentery lets it twist (sigmoid volvulus), and it is the commonest site of diverticulitis.

Nearby digestive structures Anal canal, Rectum
