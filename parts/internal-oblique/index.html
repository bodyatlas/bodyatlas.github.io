Internal oblique

Musculus obliquus internus abdominis

MuscularDeep musclesAbdomenLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Middle flat abdominal muscle, deep to the external oblique with fibres roughly at right angles to it: from the thoracolumbar fascia, anterior two-thirds of the iliac crest and lateral inguinal ligament up and forwards to the lower borders of ribs 10–12 and an aponeurosis that splits around rectus to reach the linea alba.
Function
Compresses the abdomen, flexes the trunk and rotates it to the same side (together with the opposite external oblique).
Size
About 20 cm tall, ~5 mm thick.
Clinical note
Its lowest fibres form the cremaster muscle around the spermatic cord and, with transversus, the conjoint tendon.

Nearby muscular structures Diaphragm, Quadratus lumborum, Transversus abdominis
