Extensor carpi radialis brevis

Musculus extensor carpi radialis brevis

MuscularSuperficial musclesArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Arises from the common extensor origin on the lateral epicondyle and inserts on the dorsal base of the third metacarpal.
Function
Extends and radially deviates the wrist and steadies it during gripping.
Size
About 22 cm long.
Clinical note
Its origin is the main site of degeneration in lateral epicondylitis ("tennis elbow").
