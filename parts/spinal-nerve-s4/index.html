S4 spinal nerve

Nervus spinalis S4

NervousNervesPelvisLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The S4 spinal nerve leaves the spinal canal between the fourth sacral foramina. Its sensory (dorsal) and motor (ventral) roots meet just beyond the dorsal root ganglion; the short mixed nerve then splits into a small dorsal ramus for the back muscles and skin and a larger ventral ramus, which joins the sacral plexus.
Function
Carries motor fibres to the muscles of its segment (myotome) and sensation back from its strip of skin (dermatome): the skin around the anus.
Size
About 1 mm thick; the mixed nerve is only ~1 cm long before it divides
Clinical note
Supplies the pelvic floor and external anal sphincter; the anal wink reflex tests S2-S4.
