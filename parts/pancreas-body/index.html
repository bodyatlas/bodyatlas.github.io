Body of pancreas

Corpus pancreatis

DigestiveOrgansAbdomen

View in 3D

What it is
Main central part, crossing the aorta, left adrenal and left kidney behind the stomach, from which it is separated by the lesser sac.
Function
Its acinar cells secrete about 1.5 L a day of enzyme-rich alkaline juice into the duodenum; scattered islets of Langerhans release insulin and glucagon into the blood.
Size
About 2.5 cm high and 1.5 cm thick.
Clinical note
Lying over the vertebral column, it can be crushed against the spine in blunt trauma such as steering-wheel or bicycle-handlebar injuries.
