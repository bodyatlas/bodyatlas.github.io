Medial cuneiform

Os cuneiforme mediale

SkeletalSkeletonLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The largest of the three wedge-shaped cuneiforms, at the base of the first metatarsal.
Function
Supports the big toe ray and receives tibialis anterior and fibularis longus.
Size
≈ 2.5 × 2.5 × 1.5 cm
Clinical note
Its wedge shape, like the others, helps form the transverse arch of the foot.
