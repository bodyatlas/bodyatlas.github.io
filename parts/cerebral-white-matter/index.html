Cerebral white matter

Substantia alba cerebri

NervousOrgansHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The core of each hemisphere beneath the 2-4 mm grey cortex: bundles of myelinated axons (corona radiata, internal capsule, association and commissural fibres) whose fatty myelin makes it pale.
Function
Wires cortical areas to each other, to the other hemisphere and to the thalamus, brainstem and spinal cord.
Size
About 40% of brain volume; roughly 150,000-180,000 km of myelinated fibres in a young adult
Clinical note
Multiple sclerosis strips myelin from white-matter tracts; MRI shows the plaques as bright spots around the ventricles.

Nearby nervous structures Caudate nucleus, Corpus callosum, Falx cerebri, Globus pallidus, Insula, Lateral ventricle, Parietal lobe, Putamen, Temporal lobe, Thalamus
