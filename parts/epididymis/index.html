Epididymis

Shown on the male body.

What it is
Comma-shaped organ along the back of the testis made of a single, tightly coiled duct, with a head on the upper pole, a body and a tail that becomes the vas deferens.
Function
Stores sperm and lets them mature and gain the ability to swim over about 2 weeks.
Size
About 5 cm long; the coiled duct is about 6 m long when uncoiled.
Clinical note
Epididymitis, usually from infection, causes a painful swelling behind the testis.
