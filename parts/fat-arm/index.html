Arm subcutaneous fat

Tela subcutanea membri superioris

Integumentary (skin)Fat & fasciaArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The layer of fat between the arm skin and the deep (brachial and antebrachial) fascia, carrying the cephalic and basilic veins and cutaneous nerves.
Function
Insulation, energy storage and a gliding layer over the muscles.
Size
Usually 4–15 mm; thickest over the back of the upper arm
Clinical note
Triceps skinfold thickness, pinched over the back of the upper arm, is a classic field measure of body fat.
