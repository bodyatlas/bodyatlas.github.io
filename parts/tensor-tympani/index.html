Tensor tympani

Musculus tensor tympani

Sensory (eye & ear)Deep musclesHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
A small pennate muscle lying in a bony semicanal just above the Eustachian tube; its tendon turns sharply laterally around the cochleariform process to insert on the upper part of the malleus handle.
Function
Pulls the malleus inward, tensing the eardrum and damping loud low-frequency sound and the noise of chewing.
Size
About 20-25 mm long and 1.5 mm wide.
Clinical note
Supplied by the mandibular division of the trigeminal nerve (CN V3), unlike the stapedius, which is a facial-nerve muscle.
