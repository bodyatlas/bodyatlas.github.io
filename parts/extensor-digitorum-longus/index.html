What it is
Feather-shaped (unipennate) muscle lateral to tibialis anterior, arising from the lateral tibial condyle, the upper three-quarters of the anterior fibula and the interosseous membrane.
Function
Extends the lateral four toes and dorsiflexes the ankle.
Size
Belly about 25 cm long, 2-3 cm wide.
Clinical note
Its lowest part often forms a separate slip, fibularis tertius, inserting on the fifth metatarsal.
