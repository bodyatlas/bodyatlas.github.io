Left ureter

Ureter

UrinaryOrgansWhole bodyLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Muscular tube that carries urine from the renal pelvis down along the front of psoas major, crosses the pelvic brim at the bifurcation of the common iliac artery, runs down the pelvic side wall and pierces the bladder base obliquely.
Function
Moves urine to the bladder by peristaltic waves (1–5 per minute); its oblique course through the bladder wall acts as a one-way valve against reflux.
Size
25–30 cm long, 3–5 mm in diameter.
Clinical note
Stones lodge at three narrowings: the pelviureteric junction, the pelvic brim and the vesicoureteric junction.
