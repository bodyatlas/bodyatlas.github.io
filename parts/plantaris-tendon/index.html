Plantaris tendon

Tendo musculi plantaris

MuscularDeep musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The long, ribbon-like tendon of plantaris running down between gastrocnemius and soleus, along the medial edge of the calcaneal tendon, to the posterior calcaneus.
Function
Transmits the small plantaris force to the heel.
Size
About 30 cm long, 2-3 mm wide.
Clinical note
A sudden "snap" felt in the calf was once blamed on plantaris rupture; ultrasound shows most such injuries are gastrocnemius tears.
