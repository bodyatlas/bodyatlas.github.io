Bladder trigone

Trigonum vesicae

UrinaryOrgansPelvis

View in 3D

What it is
Smooth triangular area of the bladder base whose corners are the two ureteric orifices and the internal urethral orifice; its lining is fixed to the muscle beneath, so it never forms folds.
Function
Funnels urine toward the urethra; the interureteric ridge between its upper corners guides the cystoscopist to the ureteric orifices.
Size
Each side about 2.5–3 cm.
Clinical note
It develops from the absorbed ends of the mesonephric ducts and is a common site of infection and of bladder tumours.
