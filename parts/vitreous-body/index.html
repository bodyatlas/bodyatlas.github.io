Vitreous body

Corpus vitreum

Sensory (eye & ear)OrgansHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The clear gel that fills the large cavity behind the lens (about 80% of the eye's volume). It is 99% water held in a mesh of collagen fibrils and hyaluronic acid, attached firmly at the ora serrata and optic disc.
Function
Keeps the globe inflated, holds the retina in place and transmits light to it.
Size
Volume ~4 ml; about 16-17 mm from lens to retina.
Clinical note
With age it liquefies and can peel off the retina (posterior vitreous detachment), causing floaters; vitreous samples are used in forensic medicine.
