Vastus medialis

Musculus vastus medialis

MuscularSuperficial musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The teardrop-shaped bulge above and inside the knee. It arises from the intertrochanteric line and medial lip of the linea aspera; its lowest, almost horizontal fibres (vastus medialis obliquus) insert on the medial border of the patella and the quadriceps tendon.
Function
Extends the knee and holds the patella medially in its groove during the last degrees of extension.
Size
About 30 cm long; the teardrop is about 7 cm wide and 3 cm thick above the knee.
Clinical note
Wastes quickly after knee injury or surgery; weakness of its oblique fibres is linked to patellofemoral pain and lateral patellar maltracking.
