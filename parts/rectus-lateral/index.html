Lateral rectus

Musculus rectus lateralis

Sensory (eye & ear)Deep musclesHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
A flat strap muscle arising from the common tendinous ring at the orbit apex and running forward along the orbital wall to insert by a thin, broad, white tendon on the sclera in front of the equator.
Function
Rotates the eye outward (abduction), away from the nose.
Size
~40 mm long; tendon ~8.8 mm long, inserting ~6.9 mm from the limbus.
Clinical note
The only muscle of the abducens nerve (CN VI, "LR6"); a sixth-nerve palsy - often the first sign of raised intracranial pressure - leaves the eye unable to look outward.
