What it is
Deep cup-shaped socket on the outer hip bone, formed where the ilium, ischium and pubis fuse (the triradiate cartilage in children).
Function
Holds the femoral head in the hip joint, facing outward, downward and forward.
Size
≈ 5–5.5 cm across, 2.5–3 cm deep
Clinical note
Shallow acetabula (developmental dysplasia of the hip) are screened for in newborns.

Nearby skeletal structures Coccyx, Ilium, Ischium, Pubic symphysis, Pubis, Sacrum
