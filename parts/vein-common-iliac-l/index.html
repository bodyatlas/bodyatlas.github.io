Left common iliac vein

Vena iliaca communis sinistra

CardiovascularBlood vesselsPelvisLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
Formed in front of the sacroiliac joint by the external and internal iliac veins; the longer left vein runs obliquely to the right, behind the right common iliac artery, to unite with its partner at L5, to the right of the aortic bifurcation, as the inferior vena cava.
Function
Drains the pelvis and lower limb into the inferior vena cava.
Size
~7 cm long, ~1.5 cm wide.
Clinical note
Receives the median sacral vein; compressed against L5 by the right common iliac artery in May–Thurner syndrome.
