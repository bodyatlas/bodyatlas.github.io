Optic nerve sheath

Vagina externa nervi optici

Sensory (eye & ear)NervesHeadLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The three meningeal layers (dura, arachnoid, pia) that wrap the optic nerve from the brain to the eyeball, where the dura blends with the sclera. The subarachnoid space inside it contains cerebrospinal fluid.
Function
Protects the nerve and carries CSF pressure forward to the back of the eye.
Size
Outer diameter ~4.5-5 mm.
Clinical note
An optic nerve sheath diameter above ~5-6 mm on ultrasound suggests raised intracranial pressure.
