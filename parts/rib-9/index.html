Ninth rib

Costa spuria IX

What it is
The ninth rib, a false (vertebrochondral) rib: a curved flattened bar whose head articulates with T9 and the vertebra above, its tubercle with the T9 transverse process, sloping down and forward to its costal cartilage.
Function
Protects the thoracic organs and moves up and out with the intercostal muscles during breathing (bucket-handle motion).
Size
Cross-section ≈ 6 × 11 mm; arc length ≈ 25 cm
Clinical note
Rib fractures are most common at the angle of the rib, the weakest point of the curve.
