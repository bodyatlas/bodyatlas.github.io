Main pancreatic duct

Ductus pancreaticus (Wirsung)

DigestiveOrgansAbdomen

View in 3D

What it is
Main duct running the length of the pancreas from tail to head, collecting from side branches like a herringbone, then joining the common bile duct in the hepatopancreatic ampulla (of Vater).
Function
Carries pancreatic juice to the duodenum through the major duodenal papilla.
Size
About 2 mm wide in the tail widening to 3-4 mm in the head.
Clinical note
A gallstone stuck at the shared ampulla can block it and trigger acute pancreatitis.
