Fibularis brevis

Musculus fibularis (peroneus) brevis

MuscularDeep musclesLegLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Lies deep to fibularis longus, arising from the lower two-thirds of the lateral surface of the fibula.
Function
Everts the foot and weakly plantarflexes the ankle.
Size
Belly about 15 cm long.
Clinical note
Its tendon can avulse the tuberosity of the fifth metatarsal during an inversion sprain ("pseudo-Jones" fracture).
