Anal canal

Canalis analis

DigestiveOrgansPelvis

View in 3D

What it is
Last 3-4 cm of the gut. At the anorectal junction the puborectalis sling pulls the gut forward, and the canal then runs down and back through the pelvic floor to the anus.
Function
Keeps faeces and gas in with the internal (involuntary) and external (voluntary) anal sphincters, relaxing during defecation.
Size
About 3-4 cm long.
Clinical note
The pectinate (dentate) line marks an embryological boundary: haemorrhoids above it are painless, those below it painful, because of different nerve supply.

Nearby digestive structures Rectum, Sigmoid colon
