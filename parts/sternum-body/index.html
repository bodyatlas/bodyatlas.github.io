Body of sternum

Corpus sterni

SkeletalSkeletonThorax

View in 3D

What it is
The long flat middle part of the breastbone, formed from four fused segments (sternebrae), with notches for costal cartilages 2–7.
Function
Forms the front of the rib cage and shields the heart; ribs 2–7 attach to it through their cartilages.
Size
≈ 100 mm long, 25–35 mm wide, 10 mm thick
Clinical note
Red marrow persists here throughout life, so it is a site for bone-marrow aspiration; it is split for open-heart surgery.
