Right lung, middle lobe

Lobus medius pulmonis dextri

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxRight side

View in 3D

What it is
A wedge-shaped lobe at the front and lower part of the right lung, between the horizontal fissure (4th costal cartilage) and the oblique fissure.
Function
Gas exchange; ventilated by the middle lobe bronchus through its lateral and medial segments.
Size
The smallest lobe, roughly 10% of total lung volume.
Clinical note
Its long, narrow bronchus makes it prone to collapse (middle lobe syndrome); consolidation here blurs the right heart border on a chest X-ray (silhouette sign).

Part of Visceral pleura
