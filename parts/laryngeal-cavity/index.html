Laryngeal cavity

Cavitas laryngis

RespiratoryOrgansNeck

View in 3D

What it is
The air space inside the larynx from the inlet behind the epiglottis to the lower border of the cricoid, pinched at the vocal folds (glottis) into an upper vestibule, the small lateral ventricles and a lower infraglottic cavity.
Function
Air passage between pharynx and trachea; the glottis acts as the valve controlling voice and airway protection.
Size
About 5 cm long; the glottis is its narrowest part in adults.
Clinical note
The laryngeal ventricles between the true and false vocal folds contain mucous glands that keep the vocal folds lubricated.
