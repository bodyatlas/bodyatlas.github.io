Middle nasal turbinate

Concha nasalis media

What it is
Middle nasal concha (turbinate): a scroll of thin bone covered by thick, vascular mucosa projecting from the lateral wall of the nasal cavity.
Function
Increases the mucosal surface and creates turbulent airflow so inhaled air is warmed, humidified and filtered.
Size
Part of the ethmoid bone, about 4 cm long; the frontal, maxillary and anterior ethmoid sinuses drain beneath it into the middle meatus.
Clinical note
The middle meatus (ostiomeatal complex) is the key drainage pathway targeted by functional endoscopic sinus surgery.
