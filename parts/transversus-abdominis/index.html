Transversus abdominis

Musculus transversus abdominis

MuscularDeep musclesAbdomenLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
Deepest flat abdominal muscle, with horizontal fibres running from the inner surfaces of costal cartilages 7–12, the thoracolumbar fascia, the iliac crest and the lateral inguinal ligament forwards into an aponeurosis that ends in the linea alba.
Function
Acts like a corset, compressing the abdominal contents and stiffening the lumbar spine just before limb movements.
Size
About 20 cm tall, 2–4 mm thick.
Clinical note
The TAP (transversus abdominis plane) block places local anaesthetic between it and the internal oblique, where the lower intercostal nerves run.

Nearby muscular structures Diaphragm, Internal oblique, Quadratus lumborum
