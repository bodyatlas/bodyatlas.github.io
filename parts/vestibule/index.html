What it is
The central chamber of the bony labyrinth, between the cochlea in front and the semicircular canals behind, with the oval window in its lateral wall. It contains the two otolith organs, the utricle and the saccule.
Function
Houses the gravity and linear-acceleration sensors and connects the cochlea to the semicircular canals.
Size
About 4 mm wide, 5 mm long and 3 mm deep.
Clinical note
Its medial wall borders the internal acoustic meatus, through which the vestibular nerves leave for the brainstem.
