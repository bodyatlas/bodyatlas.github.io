Right apical segmental bronchus (B1)

Bronchus segmentalis apical

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxRight side

View in 3D

What it is
Segmental (tertiary) bronchus B1, supplying the apical bronchopulmonary segment of the right upper lobe.
Function
Conducts air to one bronchopulmonary segment, a pyramidal unit of lung with its own bronchus and segmental artery.
Size
About 4 cm long and 4–6 mm in diameter; its wall holds irregular cartilage plates.
Clinical note
The apical segments of the upper lobes are the classic site of post-primary (reactivation) tuberculosis.
