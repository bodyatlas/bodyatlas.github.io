Left main bronchus

Bronchus principalis sinister

RespiratoryOrgansThoraxLeft side

View in 3D

What it is
The left main bronchus runs more horizontally (about 45°), passing under the aortic arch and in front of the oesophagus and descending aorta to reach the left hilum.
Function
Carries air into the left lung, dividing at the hilum into the upper and lower lobe bronchi.
Size
About 5 cm long and 1.2 cm in diameter.
Clinical note
An enlarged left atrium or an aortic arch aneurysm can compress it; its length suits left-sided double-lumen tubes used in thoracic surgery.
