Capitate

Os capitatum

SkeletalSkeletonArmLeft and right

View left in 3D View right in 3D

What it is
The largest carpal bone, in the centre of the distal row, with a rounded head fitting into the lunate and scaphoid.
Function
Distal-row carpal: forms a rigid unit with the metacarpals and transmits grip forces to the forearm.
Size
≈ 13 × 19 × 15 mm
Clinical note
It is the first carpal to ossify, in the first months of life.
