Proximal convoluted tubule (magnified)

Tubulus contortus proximalis

UrinaryOrgansWhole body

View in 3D

What it is
Longest part of the tubule: a coiled segment near its own corpuscle, then a straight part heading into the medulla, lined by cube-shaped cells with a dense brush border of microvilli.
Function
Reabsorbs about two-thirds of the filtered salt and water and almost all glucose, amino acids and bicarbonate, and secretes drugs and organic acids.
Size
About 14 mm long, 50–60 µm across.
Clinical note
Glucose spills into the urine once its transporters saturate (blood glucose ~10 mmol/L); SGLT2-inhibitor drugs block glucose uptake here.
