      Details

      A free 3D atlas of the human body. Peel it through ten layers from skin to skeleton, across every body system and both eyes and ears. Each of the 1,285 parts has its English and Latin name, function, size and a clinical note.

      How to explore

      Hover any structure for its name; click it for the full card.

      • Drag to rotate, right-drag or shift-drag to pan, scroll to zoom toward the cursor.
      • Double-click a structure to fly to it.
      • Drag the depth slider to peel the body away layer by layer. Ghost keeps the peeled layers as faint shells.
      • Cut slices the body along any axis; Focus jumps to an organ or region.
      • Search by English or Latin name.
